## Adaptation notes

- `argyres-global-susy-2001`: General discussions of the super-Poincare algebra, particle representations, positivity, and R symmetry were reorganized into proofs using the notation and normalizations of Chapter 25. No source solutions were reproduced.
- `argyres-global-susy-2001-ch26`: The official four-component graduate notes were used as a provenance anchor for new exercises on general and chiral superfields, Kahler transformations, moduli, discrete transformations, and improved currents. Every prompt and derivation was rewritten in the conventional four-component notation of Volume III.
- `argyres-global-susy-2001-ch27`: The author's official four-component graduate notes were used as a provenance anchor for new exercises on vector superfields, Wess-Zumino gauge, Abelian and non-Abelian field strengths, Fayet-Iliopoulos terms, Higgsing, and Coulomb-branch surface charges. Prompts and complete solutions were independently written in Volume III conventions.
- `bilal-introduction-susy-2001`: The algebra, representation, extended-supersymmetry, and BPS themes were separated into new derivational exercises on central charges, massive multiplets, shortening, and parity. Solutions were independently derived in local conventions.
- `bilal-introduction-susy-2001-ch26`: Public graduate-lecture discussions of superspace, chiral actions, F-term breaking, the goldstino, and nonlinear sigma models were separated into new component and proof exercises. Solutions were independently derived and translated into the chapter's Majorana conventions.
- `bilal-introduction-susy-2001-ch27`: Public graduate lectures on supersymmetric gauge actions, spontaneous and soft breaking, holomorphy, N=2 Yang-Mills theory, Coulomb branches, and BPS states informed new chapter-specific derivations. All prompts were adapted and credited; all solutions were independently derived in conventional four-component notation.
- `chung-et-al-soft-susy-2005-ch28`: The public review's treatments of soft flavor tensors, mass insertions, lepton-flavor violation, physical CP phases, dipole moments, and R-parity violation informed seven new exercises. Prompts and derivations were independently composed and matched to Chapter 28 notation.
- `drees-introduction-susy-1996-ch28`: The public graduate lectures on the MSSM field content, electroweak symmetry breaking, Higgs masses and mixing, radiative corrections, and chargino mixing were reorganized into eight chapter-specific derivations. All wording and complete solutions are original to this exercise edition.
- `giudice-rattazzi-gauge-mediation-1999-ch28`: The verified CERN review supplied provenance for seven independent exercises on messenger spectra, holomorphic gaugino thresholds, scalar Casimirs, unified messenger indices, flavor universality, the gravitino LSP, and the mu-Bmu problem. No source exercise or solution text was copied.
- `hls-1975-supergenerators`: The classification argument and graded-algebra constraints were recast as self-contained matrix, sign, Lorentz-spin, and three-dimensional central-charge exercises. Wording and derivations are original to this edition.
- `martin-susy-primer-v7`: The introductory particle-content discussion was used only as a provenance anchor for new helicity-construction exercises on chiral and vector multiplets. The local prompts and solutions were not copied from the notes.
- `martin-susy-primer-v7-ch26`: The verified version-7 sections on chiral Lagrangians, superspace, nonrenormalizable operators, and supersymmetry breaking were used to design independent dimensional-analysis and component exercises. All notation was converted to Volume III's conventional four-component formalism.
- `martin-susy-primer-v7-ch27`: The verified public primer was used as a provenance anchor for independently designed component exercises on gauge actions, auxiliary fields, Fayet-Iliopoulos breaking, goldstino mixing, supertraces, and soft terms. Notation and derivations were converted throughout to the four-component conventions of Volume III.
- `martin-susy-primer-v7-ch28`: The author's verified version-7 primer informed eight new exercises on matter parity, unification thresholds, gauge-mediated gaugino ratios, radiative electroweak breaking, neutralinos, sfermion mixing, and LSP stability. Prompts and worked solutions were independently written in Volume III's conventional four-component notation.
- `tong-supersymmetric-field-theory-2022`: Graduate-lecture material on algebra representations, CPT completion, extended multiplets, and BPS bounds was converted into chapter-specific counting and proof problems. Prompts, notation, and all worked solutions were independently written.
- `tong-supersymmetric-field-theory-2022-ch26`: The official Part III lecture material on chiral superfields, F- and D-terms, Wess-Zumino models, nonlinear sigma models, and O'Raifeartaigh breaking informed new chapter-specific calculations. No source problem or solution text was reproduced.
