      Using the definition of the supercovariant derivatives

                   Dα ≡ ∂α + i(σ µ )αβ̇ θ̄ β̇ ∂µ       D̄α̇ ≡ ∂¯α̇ + iθ β (σ µ )β α̇ ∂µ

superpotential and Kähler potential as a function of X and Y .

       Consider N = 1 supergravity with three chiral superfields S, T, and C. In Planck
units, the Kähler potential and superpotential are given by

                      K = − log (S + S ∗ ) − 3 log (T + T ∗ − CC ∗ )
                      W   = C 3 + a e−αS + b ,

where a, b are arbitrary complex numbers and α > 0. Compute the scalar potential. Find
the auxiliary field for S, T, C and verify that supersymmetry is broken. Assuming that C
denotes a matter field with vanishing vev, find a minimum of the potential. Are there flat
directions? A typical Kähler potential derived from string compactifications includes the
term
                                      K = −3 log Γ(τi )
where Γ is a homogeneous function of degree one of moduli fields τi . Using the homogeneity
equations τi Γi = Γ and τi Γij = 0 (where Γi = ∂Γ/∂τi , etc.) show that

                          τi Kij = 3Γj /Γ        −1
                                             Γi Kij Γj /Γ2 = 1/3

and deduce from this that if the superpotential does not depend on the τi fields then the
corresponding contribution to the N = 1 supergravity scalar potential V vanishes.
