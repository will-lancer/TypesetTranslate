  \caption{A spacetime diagram of a spacetime \((\mathbb R^4,g_{ab})\) conformal to
  Minkowski spacetime \((\mathbb R^4,\eta_{ab})\), i.e., we have
  \(g_{ab}=\Omega^2\eta_{ab}\) (see appendix~D). The conformal factor \(\Omega\) is
  chosen to be spherically symmetric with \(\Omega=1\) for \(r>1\). From the spherical
  symmetry of \(\Omega\), it follows that the curve labeled \(r=0\) is a timelike geodesic
  of \(g_{ab}\). If we choose \(\Omega\) so that \(\Omega(r=0,t)\to0\) sufficiently rapidly as
  \(t\to\infty\), this timelike geodesic will be incomplete. However, all spacelike and
  null geodesics simply ``pass through'' the world tube of nonzero curvature and thus are
  complete.}
