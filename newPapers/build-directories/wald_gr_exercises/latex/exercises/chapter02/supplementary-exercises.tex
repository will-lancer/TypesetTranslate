\SupplementaryExercise{1}{Frobenius and hypersurface orthogonality}{Adapted from the Weinberg GR exercise edition, Chapter 4; source label: Cambridge Part III General Relativity examples.}{wgr-ch4-frobenius}
Let \(u_a\) be a nowhere-vanishing one-form.  Prove locally that
\(u_a=f\nabla_at\) for some \(f,t\) if and only if \(u\wedge\dd u=0\).
Write the condition in index notation and relate it to the vorticity of a
timelike congruence.
\end{enumerate}
