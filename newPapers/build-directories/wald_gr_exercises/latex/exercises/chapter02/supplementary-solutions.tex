\exerciseeditionsection{Solutions to Supplementary Problems}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.8em,itemsep=1.2em]
\SupplementarySolution{1}{Frobenius and hypersurface orthogonality}
If \(u=f\dd t\), then \(u\wedge\dd u=f\dd t\wedge\dd f\wedge\dd t=0\).
Conversely, \(u\wedge\dd u=0\) says that the distribution annihilated by
\(u\) is integrable.  Frobenius' theorem supplies local leaves \(t=\) constant,
so their normal is proportional to \(u\).  In indices the condition is
\(u_{[a}\nabla_bu_{c]}=0\).  For normalized timelike \(u^a\), its spatial
projection is equivalent to vanishing vorticity
\(\omega_{ab}=h_a{}^ch_b{}^d\nabla_{[c}u_{d]}\).
\end{enumerate}
