\SupplementaryExercise{1}{Noether current and Komar charge}{Adapted from the Weinberg GR exercise edition, Chapter 12; source label: Cambridge Part III 2022 exam.}{wgr-ch12-noether-current}
For a generally covariant Lagrangian, vary the fields by a Lie derivative
along \(\xi^a\).  Derive the on-shell conserved Noether current and explain
why it is locally the divergence of an antisymmetric charge two-form.  Identify
that form for Einstein gravity up to normalization.
\end{enumerate}
