\exerciseeditionsection{Solutions to Supplementary Problems}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.8em,itemsep=1.2em]
\SupplementarySolution{1}{Jacobi fields and conjugate points}
For unit-speed arclength \(s\), a transverse Jacobi component obeys
\[
 J''+\frac1{a^2}J=0.
\]
The field vanishing at \(s=0\) is \(J=C\sin(s/a)\).  Its next zero occurs at
\(s=\pi a\), the antipodal point.  A nonzero Jacobi field vanishing at both
endpoints supplies a variation through nearby geodesics; past that conjugate
point the original segment is no longer a local length minimizer.
\end{enumerate}
