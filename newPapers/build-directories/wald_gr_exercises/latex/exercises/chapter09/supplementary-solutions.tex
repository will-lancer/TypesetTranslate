\exerciseeditionsection{Solutions to Supplementary Problems}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.8em,itemsep=1.2em]
\SupplementarySolution{1}{Homogeneous dust collapse}
The surface becomes trapped when its areal radius satisfies \(R(\tau)=2M\).
Inside the star an outgoing null expansion becomes negative when
\(2m(\chi)/R(\tau,\chi)>1\).  This inequality is reached at positive areal
radius, before the \(R=0\) singularity.  Comoving dust reaches the horizon and
singularity in finite proper time.  The exterior Schwarzschild coordinate
sends the surface's horizon crossing to \(t\to\infty\).
\end{enumerate}
