\SupplementaryExercise{1}{The transverse--traceless projector}{Adapted from the Weinberg GR exercise edition, Chapter 10; source label: Tong, Cambridge Part III Sheet 4.}{wgr-ch10-tt-projector}
For a wave propagating along a spatial unit vector \(n^i\), construct the
projector that maps a symmetric tensor \(S_{ij}\) to its transverse--traceless
part.  Verify transversality and zero trace.
