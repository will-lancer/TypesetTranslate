\SupplementaryExercise{1}{Velocity space as a hyperboloid}{Adapted from the Weinberg GR exercise edition, Chapter 2; source label: Tong, Cambridge Part III Sheet 1.}{wgr-ch2-velocity-hyperboloid}
The future unit four-velocities obey \(u_au^a=-1\).  Parametrize this
hyperboloid by a rapidity \(\chi\) and a direction \(\widehat{\bm n}\), derive
its induced metric, and show that the distance between two four-velocities is
their relative rapidity.
\end{enumerate}
