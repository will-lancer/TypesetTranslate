\SupplementaryExercise{1}{A conserved current from a Killing field}{Adapted from the Weinberg GR exercise edition, Chapter 5; source label: Tong, Cambridge Part III Sheet 3.}{wgr-ch5-killing-current}
Let \(T^{ab}\) be symmetric and conserved, and let \(\xi^a\) be Killing.
Prove that \(J^a=T^{ab}\xi_b\) is conserved.  State conditions under which
the associated hypersurface integral is independent of the hypersurface.
\end{enumerate}
