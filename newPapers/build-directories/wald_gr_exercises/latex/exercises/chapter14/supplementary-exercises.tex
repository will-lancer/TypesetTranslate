\SupplementaryExercise{1}{A single-mode squeezed vacuum}{Editorial problem modeled on the worked-exercise format of the Weinberg QFT and Weinberg GR editions.}{editorial-ch14-squeezed-vacuum}
Let \(b=\cosh r\,a-\sinh r\,a^\dagger\), with \([a,a^\dagger]=1\).
Verify \([b,b^\dagger]=1\).  If \(b|0_b\rangle=0\), compute the expectation
of the \(a\)-particle number in \(|0_b\rangle\).
