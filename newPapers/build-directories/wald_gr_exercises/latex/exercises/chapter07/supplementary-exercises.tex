\SupplementaryExercise{1}{Static versus stationary geometry}{Adapted from the Weinberg GR exercise edition, Chapter 13; source label: Tong, Cambridge Part III Sheet 3.}{wgr-ch13-static-stationary}
Let \(\xi^a\) be a timelike Killing field.  Prove that the geometry is locally
static precisely when \(\xi_{[a}\nabla_b\xi_{c]}=0\).  Explain how the
condition gives stationary coordinates with \(g_{0i}=0\).
\end{enumerate}
