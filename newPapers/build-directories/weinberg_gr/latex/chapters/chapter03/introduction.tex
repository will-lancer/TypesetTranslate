The Principle of the Equivalence of Gravitation and Inertia tells us how an
arbitrary physical system responds to an external gravitational field. We
shall first see what this principle says, and then in the balance of this
chapter we shall take a look at a few of its consequences. However, the
appropriate mathematical technique for implementing the Principle of
Equivalence is tensor analysis, and only after we complete the introduction
to tensor analysis in the next chapter will we be able to make use of the
full content of this principle.
