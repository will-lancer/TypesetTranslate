# Volume I errata

This file records corrections applied to the modernized transcription of
Steven Weinberg's *The Quantum Theory of Fields, Volume I: Foundations*.
The same source corrections are mirrored in the exercise edition.

## 2026-08-01 audit

The audit compared the transcription with the printed source, checked all
LaTeX labels and references, searched for duplicated words and malformed
articles, and rebuilt the complete volume. Five unambiguous prose errors were
corrected; no equation change was made.

- Section 3.8: removed the duplicated word in “the the `eigenphase'.”
- Section 5.7: corrected “theories of a charged massive particles” to
  “theories of charged massive particles,” and “a electromagnetic” to “an
  electromagnetic.”
- Section 7.7: corrected “an redundant coupling” to “a redundant coupling.”
- Appendix 7.A: restored the missing verb in “constraints can be `solved'.”
- Section 12.2: corrected “a unconventional renormalization point” to “an
  unconventional renormalization point.”
