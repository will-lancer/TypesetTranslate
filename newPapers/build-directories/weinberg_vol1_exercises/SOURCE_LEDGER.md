## Adaptation notes

- `annigoni-moretti-1205.1372`: Adapted the review of the Galilei central extension and Bargmann superselection rule into calculations of the boost-translation cocycle, the group-commutator loop, mass superselection, and the nonrelativistic contraction. The explicit wavefunction conventions and all worked solutions were supplied independently.
- `eth-qft1-hs11-sheet4`: Recast the differential-operator and generator computations as one-particle representation exercises, added finite translations and Hermiticity checks, and converted all signs and index positions to the local mostly-plus convention. Solutions were independently written.
- `harlow-qft1-2024`: Adapted the author-posted treatment of asymptotic states, cross sections, relative velocity, unitarity, the optical theorem, two-particle completeness, and one-dimensional delta-potential scattering into fourteen chapter-specific exercises. Rewritten in Weinberg's mostly-plus metric, delta-normalized states, and explicit in/out helper notation; partial-wave and K-matrix continuations were independently developed and all solutions were written independently.
- `harlow-qft1-linked-2024`: Adapted the author-posted linked-diagram and connected-correlator discussion into six exercises on partition inversion, mixed cumulants, diagram exponentiation, functional derivatives, and volume scaling. The problems were reformulated for scattering-theory cluster decomposition, and all derivations and solutions were independently written.
- `kaplunovsky-sl2c-2016`: Adapted the optional exercises on the two commuting angular-momentum algebras, the SL(2,C) cover, and bispinors into four self-contained problems, including the topology of a 2pi rotation. Rewritten for the book's notation and mostly-plus metric; solutions were independently written.
- `kit-ettp-ws21-sheet5`: Separated the Pauli-Lubanski calculation into orthogonality, rest-frame spin, and transformation-law problems. Used a one-half-normalized Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector named mathcal W to avoid collision with the book's little-group symbol and converted the Casimir sign to the mostly-plus metric. Solutions were independently written.
- `mit-823-s08-ps4`: Adapted the university-posted regulated Fourier-transform exercise into two applications to the i0 prescription and the conversion of old-fashioned energy denominators into ordered Dyson times. The distributional limits were specialized to the chapter's conventions and all solutions were independently written.
- `mit-823-s08-ps5`: Adapted the university-posted coherent-state problem into six exercises on normalized eigenstates, overcompleteness, uncertainties, number statistics, displacement operators, and the relation between in- and out-vacua in a source. Conventions were converted to the local Fock-space notation and all solutions were independently written.
- `mit-823-s23-ps1`: Split the source's Lorentz-invariance problem into independent exercises on the four-dimensional delta function, on-shell measure, invariant delta kernel, wave-packet norm, and explicit boost Jacobian. Rewritten for the book's mostly-plus metric and local state-normalization conventions; all solutions were derived independently.
- `preskill-ph205-ch3`: Adapted the discussion of Poincare representations and discrete symmetries into a focused parity/time-reversal helicity exercise, using the book's definitions and phase-independent conclusions. The solution was independently written.
- `preskill-ph236c-ch1`: Adapted the induced-representation and little-group discussion into calculations of the null little-group algebra, helicity phases, continuous-spin exclusion, Wigner rotations, spin-one mixing, and the 2+1-dimensional null stabilizer. Prompts were substantially rewritten in local notation and all solutions were independently derived.
- `tong-qft-connected-2006`: Adapted the author-posted Part III lecture notes into six exercises on vacuum-bubble cancellation, connected versus one-particle-irreducible graphs, delta-function counting, local and bilocal kernels, and four-particle factorization. The graph arguments were translated into the chapter's Hamiltonian and cluster language; solutions were independently supplied.
- `tong-qft-sheet4-2007`: Adapted the author-posted sheet into four focused exercises on connected 3-to-3 trees, exponentiation and cancellation of vacuum bubbles, invariant flavor tensors, and the exchange sign for identical fermions. The prompts were rewritten to emphasize Chapter 3 scattering structure, and the combinatorics and solutions were independently supplied.
- `woit-qftmath-ch11`: Adapted the orbit/little-group classification and Casimir discussion into problems on massive stabilizers, normalization, the limitations of the Casimirs, and massive representations in 2+1 dimensions. Specialized general constructions where stated, changed to the local mostly-plus metric, and independently wrote all solutions.
- `zhou-stanford-phys330-ps3-2022`: Adapted the author-posted Stanford exercises into six problems on impulsively generated coherent fields, classical expectation values, source radiation statistics, spacelike correlation versus causal response, factorization for separated sources, and Fourier decay of connected kernels. The prompts were specialized to cluster decomposition and all solutions were independently written in the mostly-plus convention.
- `zhou-stanford-phys330-ps4-2022`: Adapted the author-posted Stanford set into six exercises on Wick expansion, connected vacuum graphs, parity of vacuum orders, normalization by the vacuum functional, fully connected external functions, and symmetry factors. Prompts were reorganized around Chapter 4's connected-component logic, and every solution was independently derived.
- `zhou-stanford-phys330-ps5-2022`: Adapted the author-posted Stanford problems into exercises on Mandelstam kinematics, two-body decay, heavy exchange and its low-energy contact interaction, flavor tensors, identical-particle cross sections, decay multiplicity, resonance phase motion, and isospin charge channels. Prompts were substantially recast for the local chapter and metric conventions; solutions were independently derived.
