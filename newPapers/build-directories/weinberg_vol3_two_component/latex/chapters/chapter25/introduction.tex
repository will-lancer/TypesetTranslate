This chapter will develop the form of the supersymmetry algebra from first
principles, following the treatment of Haag, Lopuszanski, and
Sohnius~\hyperref[ch25-ref-1]{[1]}. As we shall see, under conditions in which
the Coleman--Mandula theorem applies, this structure is almost uniquely fixed
by the requirements of Lorentz invariance. The supermultiplet structure of
one-particle states will then be deduced directly from the supersymmetry
algebra.
