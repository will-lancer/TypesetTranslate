\chapterbackmatter{Bibliography}

\noindent\textbf{Assorted Books on General Relativity}
\begin{itemize}
  \item C. R. Adler, M. Bazin, M. Schiffer,
  \emph{Introduction to General Relativity}
  (McGraw-Hill, New York, 1965).

  \item J. L. Anderson, \emph{Principles of Relativity Physics}
  (Academic Press, New York, 1967).

  \item P. G. Bergmann, \emph{Introduction to the Theory of Relativity}
  (Prentice-Hall, Englewood Cliffs, N.J., 1942).

  \item A. S. Eddington, \emph{The Mathematical Theory of Relativity}
  (Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, 1960).

  \item A. Einstein, \emph{The Meaning of Relativity}
  (Princeton University Press, Princeton, N.J., 1946).

  \item V. Fock, \emph{The Theory of Space, Time, and Gravitation},
  trans.\ by N. N. Kemmer (2nd rev.\ ed., Macmillan, New York, 1964).

  \item C. M\o ller, \emph{The Theory of Relativity}
  (Clarendon Press, Oxford, 1952).

  \item W. Pauli, \emph{Theory of Relativity}, trans.\ by G. Field
  (Pergamon Press, Oxford, 1958).

  \item E. Schroedinger, \emph{Space-Time Structure}
  (Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, 1950).

  \item J. L. Synge, \emph{Relativity: The General Theory}
  (Interscience Publishers, New York, 1960).

  \item H. Weyl, \emph{Space-Time-Matter}, trans.\ by H. L. Brose
  (Dover Publications, New York, 1952).
\end{itemize}

On the experimental tests of the Principle of Equivalence, see the
bibliography to Chapter~8, especially the article by Dicke.
