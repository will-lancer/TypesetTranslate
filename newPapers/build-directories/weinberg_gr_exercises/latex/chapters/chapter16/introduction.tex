\bigskip

The big-bang Friedmann model discussed in the last chapter does not at any
point come into clear conflict with observation. However, it also cannot yet be
said to have been definitely confirmed by observation. Therefore in this
chapter we shall take a brief look at some of the other cosmological models
that still compete with the ``standard'' theory.
