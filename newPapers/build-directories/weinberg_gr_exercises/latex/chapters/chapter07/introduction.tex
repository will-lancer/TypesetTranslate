Chapters~3 through~5 have provided us with one-half of a complete theory of
gravitation, that is, with a mathematical description of gravitational fields
that dictates their effects on arbitrary physical systems. In this chapter we
move on to the second half of general relativity, that is, to the differential
equations that determine the gravitational fields themselves.
