\chapterbackmatter{Bibliography}

\subsubsection*{Special Relativity}

For a more comprehensive treatment of special relativity, see any one of the
following books:
\begin{itemize}
  \item[\(\square\)] J. L. Anderson, \emph{Principles of Relativity Physics}
  (Academic Press, New York, 1967), Chapters 6--9.

  \item[\(\square\)] C. M{\o}ller, \emph{The Theory of Relativity}
  (Oxford University Press, London, 1952), Chapters I--VII.

  \item[\(\square\)] W. Pauli, \emph{Theory of Relativity}, trans.\ by
  G. Field (Pergamon Press, Oxford, 1958), Part I.

  \item[\(\square\)] W. Rindler, \emph{Special Relativity}
  (2nd ed., Oliver and Boyd, Edinburgh, 1966).

  \item[\(\square\)] J. L. Synge, \emph{Relativity: The Special Theory}
  (Interscience Publishers, New York, 1956).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Relativistic Hydrodynamics}

\begin{itemize}
  \item[\(\square\)] L. D. Landau and E. M. Lifshitz,
  \emph{Fluid Mechanics}, trans.\ by J. B. Sykes and W. H. Reid
  (Pergamon Press, London, 1959), Chapter XV.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Representations of the Lorentz Group}

\begin{itemize}
  \item[\(\square\)] G. Ya. Lyubarskii, \emph{The Application of Group Theory
  in Physics} (Pergamon Press, Oxford, 1960), Chapters XV, XVI.
\end{itemize}
