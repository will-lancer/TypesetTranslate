     LINEARIZED PERTURBATIONS OF MINKOWSKI SPACETIME
In the coordinate chart used here, t is the usual time coordinate of Minkowski spacetime.
The spatial coordinates xα are arbitrary, and the background line element is

                           ds2 = gik dxi dxk = dt2 − γαβ dxα dxβ ,

where γαβ is a 3-metric of signature (+++), Dα is the metric covariant derivative of γαβ ,
∆ is the Laplacian and ∆αβ = Dα Dβ − 31 γαβ ∆.
      The full perturbed metric is

            ds2 = (1 + 2A)dt2 − 2Bα dtdxα − (1 + 2C)γαβ + 2Eαβ dxα dxβ .

non-vanishing terms which can be generated using the symmetries.
