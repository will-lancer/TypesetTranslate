and 4-velocity U a (normalized so that Ua U a = 1) has energy momentum tensor

                                T ab = (ρ + p)U a U b − pg ab .

Appealing to a conservation law derive the relativistic Euler equation

                              (ρ + p)Ua;b U b = p,a − Ua p,b U b .


       Now assume hydrostatic equilibrium obtains i.e.,
  (a) There exists a timelike Killing vector K a such that LK gab = LK ρ = LK p = 0.
  (b) The velocity U a is proportional to K a .

                             p,a = −(ρ + p)(log(Kc K c )1/2 ),a .
