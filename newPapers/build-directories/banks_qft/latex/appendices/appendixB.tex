\BanksAppendix{Cross sections}

% BANKS-SOURCE: pdf=257 print=247 kind=prose
Here we give the standard formulae for the differential cross section of a reaction in
$D$ spacetime dimensions in which two incoming particles produce an arbitrary final
state.  We use $\eta_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(-,+,\ldots,+)$, so a positive-energy
on-shell momentum is $p=(E_{\mathbf p},\mathbf p)$ with
$p^2=-m^2$ and $E_{\mathbf p}=\sqrt{\mathbf p^2+m^2}$.  The incoming velocities are
$\mathbf v_a=\mathbf P_a/E_{\mathbf P_a}$.

% BANKS-SOURCE: pdf=257 print=247 kind=display
\[
\dd\sigma=\frac{1}{2E_{\mathbf P_1}\,2E_{\mathbf P_2}
\abs{\mathbf v_1-\mathbf v_2}}
\prod_f\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{D-1}}\frac{\dd^{D-1}\mathbf p_f}{2E_{\mathbf p_f}}
\abs{\mathcal M}^2(2\pi)^D
\delta^D\left(P_1+P_2-\sum_f p_f\right).
\]

 $\abs{\mathbf v_1-\mathbf v_2}$ is the relative velocity of the two particles in the laboratory frame. Similarly
the differential decay rate of an unstable particle is

% BANKS-SOURCE: pdf=257 print=247 kind=display
\[
\dd\Gamma=\frac{1}{2M}\prod_f\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{D-1}}\frac{\dd^{D-1}\mathbf p_f}{2E_{\mathbf p_f}}
\abs{\mathcal M}^2(2\pi)^D
\delta^D\left(P-\sum_f p_f\right).
\]
