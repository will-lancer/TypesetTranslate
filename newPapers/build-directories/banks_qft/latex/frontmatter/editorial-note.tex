\BanksSourceNote{This native reading edition follows the 2008 Cambridge source.
Its mathematics is systematically expressed with a mostly-plus metric,
$D$ spacetime dimensions where valid, bold spatial vectors, and explicit
spacing between adjacent mixed tensor indices. Four-dimensional results are
identified where they occur. These convention changes preserve the mathematical
content and remain separate from the source errata. All material labeled
``Editorial solution'' or ``Exercise'' was added for this edition and is outside
Banks's original text.}
