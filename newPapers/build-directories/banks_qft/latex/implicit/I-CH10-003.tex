\begin{exercise}[Local measure near a saddle manifold]{I-CH10-003}
Model Banks's line of instanton saddles by a smooth curve $X(a)$ in
$\mathbb R^N$.  Assume that a function $S(y)$ is constant on this curve, its
Hessian has one zero direction tangent to the curve, and its restriction to
the normal space is positive.  Sketch the curve together with its tangent and
normal directions.  Choose arc length $a$ and an orthonormal normal frame
$e_A(a)$, and introduce tubular coordinates
\[
  y=X(a)+\eta^A e_A(a).
\]
Compute the Euclidean measure through first order in $\eta$.  Show that on the
curve it factors as $\dd a\prod_A\dd\eta^A$, while curvature first changes the
Jacobian away from the curve.  Use the quadratic expansion of $S$ to explain
why the collective coordinate is integrated uniformly at Gaussian order and
why the remaining directions give ordinary Gaussian factors.
\end{exercise}
