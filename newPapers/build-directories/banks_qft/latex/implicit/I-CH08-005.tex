\begin{exercise}[A one-loop check in an $R_\alpha$ Higgs gauge]{I-CH08-005}
Consider a non-abelian Higgs model in dimensional regularization with
$d_{\rm DR}=4-2\varepsilon$ and use an $R_\alpha$ gauge.  As a representative loop calculation, isolate one
mass-degenerate complex scalar multiplet of mass $m$ in a representation $R$,
normalized by $\Tr_R(T^aT^b)=T(R)\delta^{ab}$.  Compute its one-loop
contribution to the gauge-boson two-point function, including both the scalar
bubble and seagull diagrams.  Reduce the answer to one Feynman-parameter
integral, prove transversality, and extract the $\overline{\rm MS}$ ultraviolet
pole.  Check that the result is independent of $\alpha$, has the expected
high-energy logarithm, and decouples as $k^2/m^2$ at small momentum.  Explain
where this calculation sits among the full Higgs, Goldstone, ghost, and gauge
loops of the broken theory.
\end{exercise}
