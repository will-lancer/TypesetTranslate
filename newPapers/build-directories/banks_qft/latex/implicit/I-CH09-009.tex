\begin{exercise}[The two-loop four-point function in $\phi^4$ theory]{I-CH09-009}
Use the Euclidean theory
\[
  S=\int\dd^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}x\left[\frac12(\partial\phi)^2+\frac12m^2\phi^2
  +\frac{\lambda}{4!}\phi^4\right],
  \qquad d_{\mathrm{reg}}=4-\epsilon,
\]
and dimensional regularization at nonexceptional external momenta.  Compute
the two-loop 1PI four-point function, including the iterated-bubble and
overlapping ``wineglass'' topologies in all channels, the one-loop coupling
counterterm insertions, and the external-leg subtraction required by the
two-loop self-energy.

Show that all remaining poles are momentum-independent and are removed in MS
by
\[
  \lambda_0=\mu^\epsilon\left[
  \lambda+\frac{3\lambda^2}{16\pi^2\epsilon}
  +\frac{\lambda^3}{(16\pi^2)^2}
  \left(\frac{9}{\epsilon^2}-\frac{17}{6\epsilon}\right)
  \right]+O(\lambda^4).
\]
Derive
\[
  \beta(\lambda)=-\epsilon\lambda+\frac{3\lambda^2}{16\pi^2}
  -\frac{17\lambda^3}{3(16\pi^2)^2}+O(\lambda^4),
\]
and explain how this completes the renormalization program discussed by Banks.
Check that the double pole is fixed by the one-loop subdivergence.
\end{exercise}
