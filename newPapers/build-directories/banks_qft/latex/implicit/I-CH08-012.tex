\begin{exercise}[Representations with vanishing gauge anomaly]{I-CH08-012}
For left-handed Weyl fermions in a representation $R$, define
\[
 d_R^{abc}=2\Tr_R\!\left(T^a\{T^b,T^c\}\right).
\]
Prove that $d_{\overline R}^{abc}=-d_R^{abc}$ and that the tensor vanishes
when $R$ is real or pseudoreal.  Apply this trace test to all representations
of $SU(2)$, to $SO(n)$ representations with $n\ge3$ and $n\ne6$, and to
symplectic and exceptional groups.  Explain the exceptional role of
$SO(6)\simeq SU(4)$ and distinguish the perturbative cubic anomaly from the
global anomaly of an odd number of $SU(2)$ doublets.
\end{exercise}
