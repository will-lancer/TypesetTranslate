\begin{exercise}[From iterated evolution to the Dyson series]{I-CH02-005}
Let the interaction-picture evolution operator satisfy
\[
  \ii\,\partial_t U_D(t,t_0)=W(t)U_D(t,t_0),
  \qquad U_D(t_0,t_0)=\mathbf1,
\]
where $W(t)$ is an operator whose values at distinct times need not commute.
Convert this equation to a Volterra integral equation and iterate it to all
orders. Identify the ordered integration simplex, replace it by a full
$n$-dimensional cube using the time-ordering operation, and derive the
time-ordered exponential form of $U_D$. Verify the result through second order
and in the special case $[W(t),W(t')]=0$.
\end{exercise}
