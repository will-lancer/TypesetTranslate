\begin{exercise}[Locality and the continuum block-spin field]{I-CH09-005}
Start with a finite-range spin Hamiltonian on a lattice of spacing $a$.  Divide
the lattice into blocks and constrain the block averages
\[
  \Phi_I=\frac{1}{|B_I|}\sum_{x\in B_I}s_x.
\]
Define the blocked Hamiltonian by summing over microscopic spins at fixed
$\{\Phi_I\}$, and expand it as
\[
  H_1[\Phi]=\sum_{n\geq0}\sum_{I_1,\ldots,I_n}
  K_n(I_1,\ldots,I_n)\Phi_{I_1}\cdots\Phi_{I_n}.
\]
Assume that the modes eliminated in one blocking step have connected
correlators that decay exponentially on the block scale.  Express the
$K_n$ as connected cumulants and prove that they are short-ranged.  Explain
why iterating the construction preserves quasi-locality.

After $k$ steps a block contains $B^{kd}$ microscopic spins.  Show that the
mesh of the allowed block averages tends to zero, justify replacing the
discrete sum by a functional integral over a rescaled continuous field, and
explain how a local potential can enforce the original bounded range.  Check
the argument for independent Ising spins by deriving the Gaussian central
region of their block-average distribution.
\end{exercise}
