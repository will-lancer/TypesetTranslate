\begin{exercise}[Fock normalization and one-body generators]{I-CH02-004}
Let $\varepsilon=+1$ for bosons and $\varepsilon=-1$ for fermions. Starting
from
\[
  a(\mathbf p)a^\dagger(\mathbf q)
  -\varepsilon a^\dagger(\mathbf q)a(\mathbf p)
  =\delta^{D-1}(\mathbf p-\mathbf q),
  \qquad a(\mathbf p)\ket{0}=0,
\]
and the corresponding graded relations among two creators or two annihilators,
compute the inner product of
\[
  \ket{\mathbf p_1,\ldots,\mathbf p_k}_{c}
  =c_k a^\dagger(\mathbf p_1)\cdots a^\dagger(\mathbf p_k)\ket{0}.
\]
Determine the coefficient $c_k$ that produces a factor $1/k!$ multiplying the
permutation sum, and state what follows if $c_k=1$ is used instead.

For a one-particle operator $h$, define its Fock-space lift by
\[
  \dd\Gamma(h)=\int\dd^{D-1}\mathbf p\,a^\dagger(\mathbf p)(h a)(\mathbf p).
\]
Using wave packets so that all derivatives and integrations by parts are
defined, prove that $\dd\Gamma(h)$ acts as the sum of $h$ over the particles.
Apply the result to $h=p^\mu$ and to
\[
  h_{ij}=\ii(p_i\partial_j-p_j\partial_i)
\]
to verify the momentum and rotation-generator formulas in the surrounding
Fock-space discussion. Include the total-number operator as a check. Finally,
expand the delta-normalized Lorentz transformation law for an infinitesimal
boost and recover its one-particle and Fock-space boost formulas.
\end{exercise}
