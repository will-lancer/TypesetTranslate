\begin{exercise}[The nontrivial and unique consistent anomaly]{I-CH08-013}
Let $s$ be the BRST differential and let the six-form anomaly polynomial be
$I_6=C_R\Tr F^3$.  Carry out the descent
\[
 I_6=\dd Q_5^{\,0},\qquad
 sQ_5^{\,0}+\dd Q_4^{\,1}=0,
\]
and identify $\int Q_4^{\,1}$ with the consistent four-dimensional anomaly.
Prove that, when $C_R\ne0$, no local four-dimensional action has gauge
variation $-\int Q_4^{\,1}$.  One useful check is to evaluate the third Chern
character on a closed six-manifold.  Then classify local parity-odd solutions
of the Wess--Zumino condition at ghost number one and form degree four.  Show
that every such solution is a linear combination of the descents of the
independent cubic invariant tensors, plus the variation of a local
four-dimensional functional and a total derivative.  State the locality and
perturbative assumptions, and separate this result from global anomalies.
\end{exercise}
