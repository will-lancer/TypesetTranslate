\begin{exercise}[Why a dimension-two field strength is free in a CFT]{I-CH09-018}
Assume a unitary four-dimensional CFT contains a gauge-invariant conformal
primary $F_{\mu\nu}$ whose two-point function is nonzero.  At an interacting
fixed point of the Abelian coupling, use Banks's relation
$\beta(\alpha)=\alpha\gamma_A$ to show that $F_{\mu\nu}$ retains scaling
dimension $2$.

Decompose $F_{\mu\nu}$ into the Lorentz representations $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$.
Use the four-dimensional conformal unitarity bound and its shortening
condition at $\Delta=2$ to derive the source-free Maxwell equations for the
self-dual and anti-self-dual parts.  Prove, using Hilbert-space positivity and
the operator-state correspondence, that the sector generated by
$F_{\mu\nu}$ is a decoupled free Maxwell sector.  Conclude that its correlators
obey Wick factorization and that every connected correlator with more than two
field strengths vanishes.  Check the result in Gaussian Maxwell theory and
state the assumptions needed for the shortening argument.
\end{exercise}
