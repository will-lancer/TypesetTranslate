\begin{exercise}[Topology and the Yang--Mills instanton bound]{I-CH10-014}
Let $G$ be a compact connected gauge group.  For a simple factor, choose an
embedded $SU(2)\subset G$ whose inclusion has Dynkin index one.  Use the long
exact homotopy sequence of
\[
  SU(2)\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow G/SU(2)
\]
and the fact that $\pi_3(G/SU(2))=0$ for this embedding to show that every
class in $\pi_3(G)$ has a representative whose image lies in that $SU(2)$.
Explain the minor changes for a finite cover and for a semisimple product.

In the $D=4$ Euclidean specialization, with orientation $\epsilon^{0123}=+1$, take
\[
  S_E=\frac1{2g^2}\int\dd^4x\,
  \operatorname{tr}(F_{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}),
  \qquad
  n=\frac1{16\pi^2}\int\dd^4x\,
  \operatorname{tr}(F_{\mu\nu}\widetilde F_{\mu\nu}),
\]
expand $\int\operatorname{tr}(F\mp{}^*F)^2\ge0$ and derive
\[
  S_E\ge\frac{8\pi^2}{g^2}|n|.
\]
Find the saturation condition, relate its sign to that of $n$, and use the
Bianchi identity to check that a saturated field solves the Yang--Mills
equations.
\end{exercise}
