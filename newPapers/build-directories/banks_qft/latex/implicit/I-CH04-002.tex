\begin{exercise}[Feynman rules for perturbations of Proca theory]{I-CH04-002}
Let
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal L_P=-\frac14B_{\mu\nu}B^{\mu\nu}
 -\frac{\mu^2}{2}B_\mu B^\mu,
 \qquad B_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\nu B_\mu-\partial_\mu B_\nu,
\end{equation*}
with $\mu>0$, and add an arbitrary local polynomial interaction
$\mathcal L_I(B,\partial B,\chi,\partial\chi)$, where $\chi$ denotes any
additional free bosonic or fermionic fields.  Starting from the interaction
expansion of the generating functional, derive the momentum-space
diagrammatic rules.  State the Proca propagator, the rule for an arbitrary
vertex, the momentum factors produced by derivatives, momentum conservation,
loop integration, statistics signs, symmetry factors, and the distinction
between connected and one-particle-irreducible graphs.  Include external
source legs when describing Green functions, and check the rules on a
derivative-free monomial $-\lambda C^{\mu_1\ldots\mu_n}
B_{\mu_1}\cdots B_{\mu_n}/n!$.
\end{exercise}
