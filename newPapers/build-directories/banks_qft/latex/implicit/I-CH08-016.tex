\begin{exercise}[Thermal electroweak baryon violation]{I-CH08-016}
In four-dimensional space-time, relate the electroweak anomaly to transitions between vacua with neighboring
$SU(2)$ Chern--Simons number.  At zero temperature, identify the instanton
action and its exponential suppression.  At temperature $T$, estimate the
thermal transition rate over the sphaleron barrier using
\[
 E_{\rm sph}(T)=\frac{4\pi v(T)}{g_2}
 B\!\left(\frac{\lambda}{g_2^2}\right),
\]
where $B$ is of order one.  Show how the Boltzmann exponent disappears as the
electroweak-symmetric regime is approached and quote the parametric symmetric-
phase rate.  Check its dimensions and determine the changes in $B$, $L$, and
$B-L$ per transition.  Explain why this thermal many-body process can be fast
near the electroweak scale even though few-particle baryon violation remains
invisible.
\end{exercise}
