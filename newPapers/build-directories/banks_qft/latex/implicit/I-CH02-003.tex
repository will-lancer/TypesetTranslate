\begin{exercise}[Unitarity with delta-function normalization]{I-CH02-003}
On the positive mass shell let $p^0=\omega_{\mathbf p}
=\sqrt{\mathbf p^2+m^2}$, $p^2=-m^2$, and normalize scalar one-particle states by
$\braket{\mathbf p}{\mathbf q}=\delta^{D-1}(\mathbf p-\mathbf q)$. For a proper orthochronous
Lorentz transformation $\Lambda$, prove that
\[
  U(\Lambda)\ket{\mathbf p}
  =\sqrt{\frac{\omega_{\boldsymbol{\Lambda p}}}{\omega_{\mathbf p}}}\ket{\boldsymbol{\Lambda p}}
\]
defines a unitary action. Derive the Jacobian of
$\mathbf p\mapsto\boldsymbol{\Lambda p}$ from invariance of the positive-shell
measure $\dd^{D-1}\mathbf p/(2\omega_{\mathbf p})$, use it to transform the $(D-1)$-dimensional
delta function, and check both inner products and the composition law.
\end{exercise}
