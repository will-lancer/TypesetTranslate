\begin{exercise}[From the classical action to the Schrödinger operator]{I-CH10-001}
Let the configuration space be flat, with dimensionless coordinates $q^i$, and
set $\hbar=1$.  Starting from
\[
  S=\int\dd t\left[\frac{\dot q^i\dot q^i}{2g^2}
    -\frac{V(q)}{g^2}\right],
\]
compute the canonical momentum and Hamiltonian.  Quantize in the coordinate
representation and derive
\[
  \left[-\frac{g^2}{2}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial q^i\partial q^i}
    +\frac{V(q)}{g^2}\right]\psi=E\psi.
\]
Track every factor of $g^2$.  Explain why the phase
$\psi\sim\exp(\ii W/g^2)$ gives $\partial_iW=\dot q_i$ at leading WKB order,
even though the canonical momentum obtained directly from the action contains
$1/g^2$.
\end{exercise}
