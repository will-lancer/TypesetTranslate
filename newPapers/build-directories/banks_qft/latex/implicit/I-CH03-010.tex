\begin{exercise}[Several scalar species]{I-CH03-010}
Let $\phi^a$ be real scalar fields with
\[
 \mathcal L_0=-\frac12\partial_\mu\phi^a\partial^\mu\phi^a
 -\frac12\phi^aM^2_{ab}\phi^b
\]
for a symmetric mass matrix, and take the interaction
\[
 \mathcal L_I=-\sum_{r\geq3}\frac{1}{r!}
 \lambda_{a_1\ldots a_r}\phi^{a_1}\cdots\phi^{a_r},
\]
where each coupling tensor is completely symmetric.  Derive the free
propagator matrix in momentum space and the $r$-leg vertex tensor.  Give the
rules for allowed Wick contractions, species labels on external and internal
lines, and the sums over internal indices.  Show how the rules reduce in a
mass-eigenstate basis and prove that a simultaneous orthogonal change of field
basis leaves a graph amplitude unchanged.
\end{exercise}
