\begin{exercise}[Resolvent trace from periodic Lorentzian paths]{I-CH10-016}
For $\operatorname{Im}z>0$, begin with the proper-time identity for
$(z-H)^{-1}$ and derive
\[
  \Tr\frac1{z-H}=-\ii\int_0^\infty\dd T\,
  \ee^{\ii zT}\Tr\ee^{-\ii HT}.
\]
Insert coordinate states in the trace and time-slice the evolution kernel to
obtain a Lorentzian path integral over paths satisfying $q(T)=q(0)$.  Show how
the integration over $T$ can be absorbed into a measure over periodic paths,
leading, up to the displayed normalization factor, to Banks's notation
\[
  \Tr(z-H)^{-1}=\int[\dd q(t)]\,
  \ee^{\ii T[q]z}\ee^{\ii S[q]/g^2}.
\]
State the $\ii\epsilon$ prescription, identify the role of time-translation
overcounting for a periodic saddle, and give the field-theory version with
periodic field configurations on a time interval of length $T$.
\end{exercise}
