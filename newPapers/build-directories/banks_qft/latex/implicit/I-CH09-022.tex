\begin{exercise}[RG invariance of LSZ amplitudes]{I-CH09-022}
Let a connected renormalized Green function with external particle species
$i$ obey
\[
  \mathcal D W_N=-\left(\sum_i\gamma_i\right)W_N,
\]
and suppose its two-point functions have isolated poles
\[
  W_{2,i}(p)\sim\frac{Z_i}{p^2+M_i^2-\ii0}.
\]
Use the first-order nature of $\mathcal D$ to separate the simple- and
double-pole terms and prove
\[
  \mathcal D Z_i=-2\gamma_iZ_i,
  \qquad \mathcal D M_i^2=0.
\]
Apply $\mathcal D$ to the full LSZ expression, including normalized external
wave functions, pole-removal factors, and $Z_i^{-1/2}$.  Show term by term
that the anomalous dimensions cancel and derive $\mathcal D S=0$.  State the
assumptions under which the on-shell limit commutes with the RG operation and
check the result in a free theory.
\end{exercise}
