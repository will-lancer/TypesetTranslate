\begin{exercise}[Tree-level electroweak amplitudes]{I-CH08-009}
Work in four-dimensional Minkowski space with
$\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-,+,+,+)$ and
$p\cdot x=-p^0x^0+\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf x$, and use
$e=g_2\sin\theta_W$ and
\[
 \mathcal L_{\rm CC}=-\frac{g_2}{\sqrt2}(W^+_\mu J_-^\mu+\mathrm{h.c.}),
 \qquad
 \mathcal L_Z=-\frac{g_2}{\cos\theta_W}Z_\mu
 \bar f\gamma^\mu[(T^3-Qs_W^2)P_L-Qs_W^2P_R]f.
\]
Derive the low-energy four-fermion operator for muon decay and the relation
between $G_F$, $g_2$, and $m_W$.  Compute the massless tree-level differential
and total cross sections for $e^+e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-$ from photon exchange.
Write the low-energy neutral-current operator for neutrino--electron
scattering and give the left- and right-handed electron couplings.  Finally,
use unitarity of $V_{\rm CKM}$ to explain the absence of tree-level neutral
flavor-changing currents.
\end{exercise}
