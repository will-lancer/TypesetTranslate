\begin{exercise}[Small-proper-time power counting]{I-CH09-014}
Use Banks's parametric form
\[
  \Pi(p^2)=C_{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}\int_0^\infty\dd s\,s^{1-d_{\mathrm{reg}}/2}
  \int_0^1\dd x\,A_{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}(x)
  \ee^{-s[m_0^2+x(1-x)p^2]},
\]
where $A_d(x)$ is independent of $s$.  Expand about $p^2=0$ and determine the
small-$s$ degree of the coefficient of $(p^2)^n$.  Prove that at
$d_{\mathrm{reg}}=4$ only
the constant term of $\Pi$ is ultraviolet divergent.  Translate this result
to
\[
  \Pi_{\mu\nu}(p)=(p^2\delta_{\mu\nu}-p_\mu p_\nu)\Pi(p^2)
\]
and show that every term above order $p^2$ in the tensor is UV finite.  Check
the conclusion after one derivative with respect to $p^2$.
\end{exercise}
