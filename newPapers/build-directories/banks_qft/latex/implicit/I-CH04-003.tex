\begin{exercise}[One-loop graphs for a charged scalar and a Proca field]{I-CH04-003}
Consider a free complex scalar of mass $m$, a free Proca field $B_\mu$ of
mass $\mu$, and the sole interaction
\begin{equation*}
 \mathcal L_I=\ii gB_\mu
 \bigl(\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*\bigr).
\end{equation*}
Draw every connected two-, three-, and four-point diagram through one loop.
Organize the answer by the external field content allowed by the global
phase symmetry of $\phi$: $BB$ and $\phi\phi^*$ at two points;
$BBB$ and $B\phi\phi^*$ at three points; and $BBBB$,
$BB\phi\phi^*$, and $\phi\phi\phi^*\phi^*$ at four points.  Include the
free or tree diagrams, all one-particle-irreducible loop cores, and every
one-loop self-energy or vertex insertion on a tree graph.  Orient scalar
lines, route and label all momenta, and give every symmetry factor.  Write
the corresponding loop integrals without evaluating them.  Identify
contractions that contain a one-point tadpole, explain which odd-vector
loops cancel, and state the disconnected free products that would be added
to the full Green functions.
\end{exercise}
