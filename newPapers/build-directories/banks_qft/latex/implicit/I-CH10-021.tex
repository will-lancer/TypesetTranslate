\begin{exercise}[Electromagnetic angular momentum of two dyons]{I-CH10-021}
Use Heaviside--Lorentz units in the $D=4$ Lorentzian theory.  Place two static
dyons with charges
$(e_i,g_i)$ and $(e_j,g_j)$ at distinct points $\mathbf r_i$ and
$\mathbf r_j$, and define
\[
  \widehat{\mathbf r}=
  \frac{\mathbf r_j-\mathbf r_i}{|\mathbf r_j-\mathbf r_i|}.
\]
Starting from their Coulomb electric and magnetic fields, evaluate
% CONVENTION-SCOPE: fixed D=4 reason=electromagnetic angular momentum in three spatial dimensions
\[
  \mathbf L_{\rm em}=\int\dd^3\mathbf x\,
  \mathbf x\times(\mathbf E\times\mathbf B).
\]
Separate self-terms from cross-terms and carry out the remaining spatial
integral.  Prove that the separation-independent contribution is
\[
  \mathbf L_{\rm em}=
  \frac{e_i g_j-e_j g_i}{4\pi}\,\widehat{\mathbf r}.
\]
Finally use angular-momentum quantization along
$\widehat{\mathbf r}$ to derive the Schwinger--Zwanziger condition
$e_i g_j-e_jg_i=2\pi n_{ij}$ with $n_{ij}\in\mathbb Z$.  State how the result
reduces to Dirac quantization for a purely electric particle and a purely
magnetic monopole.
\end{exercise}
