\begin{exercise}[The longitudinal form factor]{I-CH06-006}
Suppose the loop-integrated vertex has been reduced between equal-mass
on-shell spinors to
\[
 \bar u(p+q)\left[A(q^2)(2p+q)_\mu+B(q^2)\gamma_\mu
 +C(q^2)q_\mu\right]u(p).
\]
Apply current conservation to prove that the coefficient of \(q_\mu\)
vanishes.  Treat the apparent \(q^2=0\) loophole carefully.  As a check,
apply the interchange \(x\leftrightarrow y\) to the \(q_\mu\) term in the
Feynman-parameter expression immediately preceding equation~\eqref{eq:6.17}.
\end{exercise}
