\begin{exercise}[Translational zero modes of a localized field configuration]{I-CH10-008}
Let $I^a(x)$ be a localized stationary point of the $D$-dimensional Euclidean
scalar action, so that
\[
  -\partial^2 I^a+\frac{\partial V}{\partial I^a}=0.
\]
The quadratic fluctuation operator is
\[
  \mathcal M_{ab}=-\delta_{ab}\partial^2
  +\frac{\partial^2V}{\partial I^a\partial I^b}\bigg|_{I(x)}.
\]
Use the translated family $I^a(x-c)$ to identify its $D$ tangent vectors.
Then differentiate the field equation and verify by direct substitution that
$\mathcal M_{ab}\partial_iI^b=0$ for each $i=1,\ldots,D$.  For an
$O(D)$-invariant instanton, discuss linear independence, behavior at the
origin, and square integrability at infinity.
\end{exercise}
