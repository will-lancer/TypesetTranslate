\begin{exercise}[The anomaly pole and its discontinuity]{I-CH08-019}
In four-dimensional space-time, let $\Gamma^{\lambda\mu\nu}(q,p_1,p_2)$ be a three-current Green function,
with momentum $q=p_1+p_2$ entering an anomalous current and conserved vector
currents on the other legs.  Starting from
\[
 q_\lambda\Gamma^{\lambda\mu\nu}
 =C\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}p_{1\rho}p_{2\sigma},
\]
isolate the longitudinal form factor and show that it contains $1/q^2$.
Use the distributional discontinuity of a massless propagator to derive
$\operatorname{disc}\Gamma\propto\delta(q^2)$.  Show how a Nambu--Goldstone
pole realizes this term.  Then apply the Cutkosky conditions to a massless
fermion triangle and explain how its collinear on-shell configuration gives
the same zero-mass discontinuity.  Include a dimensional check and explain why
a spectrum with a positive mass gap cannot satisfy the anomalous Ward
identity within an ordinary local particle description with no additional
anomaly-matching topological sector.
\end{exercise}
