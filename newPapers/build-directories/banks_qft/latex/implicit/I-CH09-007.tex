\begin{exercise}[From metric variation to local Weyl invariance]{I-CH09-007}
Let a local, cut-off-independent QFT be coupled to a smooth background metric,
with renormalized generating functional $W[g]$ and stress tensor defined by
\[
  \delta W=\frac12\int\dd^D x\sqrt g\,
  \langle T^{\mu\nu}\rangle\delta g_{\mu\nu}.
\]
Assume that there is no Weyl anomaly and no nontrivial virial-current
obstruction, so the fixed-point metric variation vanishes for every smooth,
compactly supported local rescaling
$\delta_\sigma g_{\mu\nu}=2\sigma(x)g_{\mu\nu}$.

Prove, as an operator-valued distribution inside separated-point correlators,
that $T^\mu{}_{\mu}(x)=0$ after the allowed improvement.  Deduce invariance
under finite local Weyl transformations connected to the identity.  In flat
space, combine an infinitesimal conformal diffeomorphism with a compensating
Weyl transformation and derive conformal invariance.  Check the improvement
for a free massless scalar in $D>2$.
\end{exercise}
