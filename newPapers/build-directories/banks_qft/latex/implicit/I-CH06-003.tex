\begin{exercise}[High-energy scaling of the total cross section]{I-CH06-003}
In four spacetime dimensions, use dimensions and the scale-free massless
limit to show that the leading total cross section for
\(e^+e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-\) has the form
\(\sigma_{\rm tot}=C\alpha^2/E^2\).  Determine \(C\) from
equation~\eqref{eq:6.11}.  Rewrite that equation as \(\alpha^2/E^2\)
times a function of mass ratios, and expand it at large \(E\).  Identify
the leading corrections due to the photon and muon masses, and state where
an electron-mass correction would enter.
\end{exercise}
