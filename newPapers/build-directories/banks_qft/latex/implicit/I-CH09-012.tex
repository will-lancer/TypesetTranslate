\begin{exercise}[Gamma functions in proper-time integrals]{I-CH09-012}
Starting from
\[
  \Gamma(t)=\int_0^\infty\dd u\,u^{t-1}\ee^{-u},
  \qquad \Re t>0,
\]
derive $\Gamma(t+1)=t\Gamma(t)$ by integration by parts and hence obtain
$\Gamma(n)=(n-1)!$ for positive integers $n$.  Derive Euler's reflection
identity
\[
  \Gamma(t)\Gamma(1-t)=\frac{\pi}{\sin\pi t},
  \qquad 0<\Re t<1,
\]
and use positivity of the defining integral to show
$\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt\pi$.

Then evaluate
\[
  \int_0^\infty\dd s\,s^{a-1}\ee^{-sM^2}
\]
and the $d_{\mathrm{reg}}$-dimensional Gaussian integral used in Banks's Schwinger-parameter
calculation.  Explain their analytic continuation in $a$ and $d_{\mathrm{reg}}$, and check
the proper-time formula at $a=1$.
\end{exercise}
