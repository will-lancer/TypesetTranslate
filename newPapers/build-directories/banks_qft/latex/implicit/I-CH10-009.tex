\begin{exercise}[Unbinding threshold in the two-dimensional instanton gas]{I-CH10-009}
Banks writes the large-separation contribution of one instanton--anti-instanton
pair in the global $U(1)$ model as
\[
  Z_{I\bar I}\mathrel{\propto}
  \mathcal V\,\ee^{-2S_0/g^2}
  \int_{|\mathbf R|\ge r_+}\dd^2\mathbf R\,
  \ee^{-\frac1{g^2}\ln(|\mathbf R|/r_+)}.
\]
Evaluate the infrared radial integral.  Find the value of $g^2$ at which its
large-$|\mathbf R|$ convergence changes, and describe the borderline divergence.
Interpret the result as the competition between the logarithmic interaction
and the two-dimensional positional entropy, connecting the two regimes to the
bound-pair and plasma phases of the Kosterlitz--Thouless transition.  Use the
normalization of the interaction displayed above.
\end{exercise}
