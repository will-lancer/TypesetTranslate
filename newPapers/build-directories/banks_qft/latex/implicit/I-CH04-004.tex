\begin{exercise}[Conserved sources in the Stueckelberg description]{I-CH04-004}
In the Stueckelberg parametrization
\begin{equation*}
 B_\mu=A_\mu-\partial_\mu\theta,
 \qquad
 A_\mu\longrightarrow A_\mu+\partial_\mu\Omega,
 \qquad
 \theta\longrightarrow\theta+\Omega,
\end{equation*}
couple an external current through
$S_J=\int\dd^D x\,A_\mu J^\mu$.  Integrate by parts to find the gauge
variation of $S_J$.  Prove that invariance under arbitrary gauge parameters
of compact support requires $\partial_\mu J^\mu=0$, and prove the converse
when either $J^\mu$ or $\Omega$ vanishes sufficiently rapidly at infinity.
Under the same condition, show that
$\int J^\mu A_\mu=\int J^\mu B_\mu$ and that the longitudinal part of the
Proca propagator drops out of source amplitudes as $\mu\to0$.
\end{exercise}
