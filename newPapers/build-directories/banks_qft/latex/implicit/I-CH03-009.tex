\begin{exercise}[Feynman rules for a derivative vertex]{I-CH03-009}
Adopt the Lorentzian interaction
\[
 \mathcal L_I=-\frac{g}{2!\,2!}\,
 \phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi.
\]
Label the two differentiated field slots before performing Wick contractions.
Derive the position-space rule for a four-leg vertex at $y$, displaying every
derivative on the propagator associated with its own slot.  Sum over the
inequivalent choices of the two differentiated external or internal lines.
Fourier transform with all momenta incoming and obtain the symmetrized vertex
factor in the convention shown above.  Verify its permutation symmetry and
its simplification when momentum is conserved.
\end{exercise}
