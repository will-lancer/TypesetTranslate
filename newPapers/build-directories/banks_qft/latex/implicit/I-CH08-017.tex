\begin{exercise}[The Wess--Zumino action and anomaly matching]{I-CH08-017}
Let $U(x)\in SU(N_F)$ describe Nambu--Goldstone fields with
$U\mapsto g_LUg_R^{-1}$, and extend $U$ from four-dimensional space-time
$M_4$ to a five-manifold $B_5$ with boundary $M_4$.  Derive the ungauged
Wess--Zumino term
\[
 \Gamma_{\rm WZ}[U]=-\frac{\ii k}{240\pi^2}
 \int_{B_5}\Tr(U^{-1}\dd U)^5.
\]
Show why independence of the extension quantizes $k$, and determine $k$ for
an $SU(N)$ color theory from its ultraviolet flavor anomaly.  Explain how
coupling background fields $A_L,A_R$ and applying descent produces the gauged
Wess--Zumino action whose variation matches the quark anomaly.  Reduce the
result to a broken $U(1)$ subgroup, obtain the interaction
$\frac{K}{f}\phi F_{\mu\nu}\widetilde F^{\mu\nu}$, and relate the
dimensionless $K$ to the microscopic triangle trace.  Verify the
anomaly-matching role and the scaling of a
pseudo-Goldstone decay amplitude with $K/f$.
\end{exercise}
