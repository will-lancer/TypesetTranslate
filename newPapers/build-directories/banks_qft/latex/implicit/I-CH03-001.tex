\begin{exercise}[The free generating functional from operators]{I-CH03-001}
Let
\[
 \phi(x)=\int\frac{\dd^{D-1}\mathbf p}{\sqrt{(2\pi)^{D-1}\,2\omega_{\mathbf p}}}
 \left[a(\mathbf p)\ee^{+\ii p\mathbin{\cdot}x}+a^\dagger(\mathbf p)\ee^{-\ii p\mathbin{\cdot}x}\right],
 \qquad [a(\mathbf p),a^\dagger(\mathbf q)]=\delta^{D-1}(\mathbf p-\mathbf q),
\]
where $p^0=\omega_{\mathbf p}=\sqrt{\mathbf p^2+m^2}$.  For a real smooth
source of compact support, evaluate
\[
 Z_0[J]=\langle0|T\exp\!\left(\ii\int\dd^D x\,J(x)\phi(x)\right)|0\rangle
\]
entirely by operator methods.  Identify the contraction obtained from the
creation--annihilation decomposition, count all contractions in the $2r$th
term, and show how their sum exponentiates.  Check $Z_0[0]=1$ and recover the
time-ordered two-point function by differentiating twice with respect to $J$.
\end{exercise}
