\begin{exercise}[Baryon and lepton anomalies]{I-CH08-015}
In four-dimensional space-time, write every standard-model fermion as a left-handed Weyl field.  For one
generation, evaluate the mixed $B\,SU(2)_L^2$ and
$L\,SU(2)_L^2$ triangle coefficients, including color multiplicities.
Repeat the trace for two external hypercharge fields.  Derive the anomalous
divergences of $j_B^\mu$ and $j_L^\mu$, and show that their gauge-field terms
are equal.  Conclude that $B-L$ is conserved by standard-model gauge
interactions.  Integrate the $SU(2)$ equation over a configuration of
topological charge $\Delta N_{\rm CS}$ and find $\Delta B$ and $\Delta L$ for
$N_g$ generations.
\end{exercise}
