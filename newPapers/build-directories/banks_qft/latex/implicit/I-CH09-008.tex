\begin{exercise}[Resumming a quadratic relevant perturbation]{I-CH09-008}
Take the free massless Euclidean scalar propagator $G_0(p)=1/p^2$ and perturb
the action by
\[
  \delta S=\frac12m^2\int\dd^D x\,\phi^2(x).
\]
Derive the momentum-space insertion carried by this quadratic vertex.  Sum
all insertions on a propagator and show that the geometric series is
\[
  G_0-G_0m^2G_0+G_0m^2G_0m^2G_0-\cdots
  =\frac{1}{p^2+m^2}.
\]
Give the corresponding Minkowski formula with its $\ii\epsilon$ prescription,
state the convergence interpretation of the series, and check both the
$m^2\to0$ limit and the Dyson equation.
\end{exercise}
