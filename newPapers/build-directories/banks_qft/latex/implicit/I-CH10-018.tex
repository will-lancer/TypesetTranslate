\begin{exercise}[The collective-coordinate mass equals the static energy]{I-CH10-018}
For canonical scalar fields in one spatial dimension, use
\[
  \mathcal L=\frac1{g^2}\left[
  \frac12\dot\phi_i^2-\frac12(\partial_x\phi_i)^2-V(\phi)\right]
\]
and a finite-energy static soliton $\phi_i^c(x)$.  Promote its center to a
slow coordinate through $\phi_i(t,x)=\phi_i^c(x-X(t))$.  Expand the energy to
order $\dot X^2$ and read off the collective-coordinate mass
\[
  M_X=\frac1{g^2}\int\dd x\,
  \frac{\dd\phi_i^c}{\dd x}\frac{\dd\phi_i^c}{\dd x}.
\]
Multiply the static field equation by $\dd\phi_i^c/\dd x$ to obtain its first
integral.  With equal vacuum energies shifted to zero, prove $M_X=E_c$, where
$E_c$ is the static soliton energy.  Check the units and identify the
assumption on the fields at spatial infinity.
\end{exercise}
