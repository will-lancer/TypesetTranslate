\begin{exercise}[A soliton and its relativistic mass shell]{I-CH10-017}
In the $D=2$ Lorentzian scalar theory, with mostly-plus signature,
\[
  \mathcal L=\frac1{g^2}\left[
    -\frac12\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi-V(\phi)\right],
\]
let $\phi_c(x)$ be a localized static solution with finite rest energy $M$.
Boost it to
\[
  \phi_v(t,x)=\phi_c\bigl(\gamma(x-vt)\bigr),
  \qquad \gamma=(1-v^2)^{-1/2}.
\]
Using the stress tensor, compute the energy and momentum of this configuration.
For a kink between degenerate vacua, derive the zero-pressure first integral
$\tfrac12(\phi_c')^2=V(\phi_c)$ and use it to show
\[
  E=\gamma M,\qquad \mathbf P=\gamma Mv,\qquad
  E^2-\mathbf P^2=M^2.
\]
Then give the covariant argument in higher dimensions: explain why the
integrated stress tensor of a localized rest solution defines a $D$-momentum,
how boosts sweep out the mass shell, and how a compensating gauge
transformation may be used when a preferred gauge such as $A_0=0$ was chosen.
Connect this family to the particle pole in the semiclassical resolvent.
\end{exercise}
