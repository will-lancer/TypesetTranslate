\begin{exercise}[Stress tensors and conformal symmetry]{I-CH07-003}
Work in $D$-dimensional Minkowski space with
$\eta_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(-,+,\ldots,+)$.  Assume throughout that the
functional measure is invariant and that fields decrease fast enough for the
stated surface terms to vanish.  Establish the claims left
unproved in Banks's discussion of space-time symmetries.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item Make an infinitesimal translation local,
$\delta\phi_i=-f^\nu(x)\partial_\nu\phi_i$, and derive the translation
Ward--Takahashi identity for a time-ordered product with one insertion of
$T^{\mu\nu}$, including every contact term.

\item If
\[
 T^{\mu\nu}\mapsto T^{\mu\nu}+\partial_\lambda M^{\mu\nu\lambda},
 \qquad M^{\mu\nu\lambda}=-M^{\lambda\nu\mu},
\]
prove that conservation and the momentum
$P^\nu=\int\dd^{D-1}\mathbf x\,T^{0\nu}$ are unchanged.

\item Verify general-coordinate invariance of
\[
\begin{aligned}
 S_\phi&=\int\dd^D x\sqrt{-g}\left(
 -\frac12g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi\partial_\nu\phi-V(\phi)\right),\\
 S_M&=-\frac14\int\dd^D x\sqrt{-g}\,
 g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma}F_{\mu\nu}F_{\rho\sigma}.
\end{aligned}
\]

\item Solve the conformal Killing equation on Minkowski space.  In this part
specialize to $D=4$.  Exhibit
translations, Lorentz transformations, dilatations, and special conformal
transformations; compute their commutators and identify the resulting
fifteen-dimensional algebra with $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$.  Derive the condition
$T^\mu{}_{\mu}=0$ for conformal invariance.

\item Show that a conformally invariant theory whose one-particle masses form
a discrete spectrum can contain only massless particles.

\item Define the metric stress tensor by
\[
 \delta S=-\frac12\int\dd^D x\sqrt{-g}\,
 T_{\mathrm H\,\mu\nu}\delta g^{\mu\nu}
 =\frac12\int\dd^D x\sqrt{-g}\,
 T_{\mathrm H}^{\mu\nu}\delta g_{\mu\nu}.
\]
Prove that it is symmetric and covariantly conserved on the matter equations
of motion.  In flat space relate it to the canonical Noether tensor through
the Belinfante improvement, and prove conservation of
\[
 J^{\lambda\mu\nu}
 =x^\mu T_{\mathrm B}^{\lambda\nu}
 -x^\nu T_{\mathrm B}^{\lambda\mu}.
\]

\item Compute the Belinfante tensors of a free massless Dirac field and a
free Maxwell field in $D=4$.  Use
$\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=-2\eta^{\mu\nu}$ and verify their symmetry,
conservation, and vanishing trace on shell.

\item For a free massless real scalar, verify that
\[
 T_{\mathrm I}^{\mu\nu}=T_{\mathrm B}^{\mu\nu}
 -\frac{D-2}{4(D-1)}(\partial^\mu\partial^\nu-\eta^{\mu\nu}\partial^2)\phi^2
\]
is conserved and traceless on shell.  Show explicitly how the improvement
term changes under the constant field shift $\phi\mapsto\phi+c$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{exercise}
