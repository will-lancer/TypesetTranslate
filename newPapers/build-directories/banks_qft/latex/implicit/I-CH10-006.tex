\begin{exercise}[Node theorem and the bottom of the fluctuation spectrum]{I-CH10-006}
For a monotone one-dimensional instanton $X(t)$, consider the self-adjoint
operator
\[
  \mathcal M=-\frac{\dd^2}{\dd t^2}+V''(X(t))
\]
on square-integrable fluctuations.  Assume $X$ approaches nondegenerate vacua
exponentially and use the translation mode $\dot X$.  Apply the one-dimensional
Sturm oscillation theorem to prove that this mode is the lowest normalizable
eigenfunction and that the single-instanton Hessian has no negative
eigenvalues.  Locate the continuum threshold from the vacuum curvatures, and
state what the argument does and does not decide about positive bound states.
\end{exercise}
