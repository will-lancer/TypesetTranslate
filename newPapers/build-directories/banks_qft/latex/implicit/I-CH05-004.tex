\begin{exercise}[Berezin integration and the top monomial]{I-CH05-004}
Let $\eta_1,\ldots,\eta_n$ generate a Grassmann algebra,
$\eta_i\eta_j=-\eta_j\eta_i$.  Define a one-variable integral as a linear,
translation-invariant functional and normalize it by
\begin{equation*}
  \int\dd\eta\,\eta=1.
\end{equation*}
Show first that translation invariance forces
$\int\dd\eta\,1=0$.  Extend the definition by iteration, using left
Berezin derivatives and the convention
\begin{equation*}
  \dd^n\eta=\dd\eta_n\cdots\dd\eta_1.
\end{equation*}
Prove that $\int\dd^n\eta\,f(\eta)$ extracts the coefficient of
$\eta_1\cdots\eta_n$.  Determine the answer for a permuted top monomial and
track the sign produced by exchanging two differentials.  Check the result
explicitly for two variables.
\end{exercise}
