\begin{exercise}[Scalar one-particle matrix element]{I-CH03-015}
Use Banks's one-particle normalization
\(\langle\mathbf q|\mathbf p\rangle=\delta^{D-1}(\mathbf q-\mathbf p)\) and
\[
 U(\Lambda)|\mathbf p\rangle
 =\sqrt{\frac{\omega_{\boldsymbol{\Lambda p}}}{\omega_{\mathbf p}}}\,|\boldsymbol{\Lambda p}\rangle.
\]
Assume that the vacuum is invariant and that $\Phi(x)$ is a local Lorentz
scalar with a nonzero matrix element between the vacuum and a stable scalar
particle of mass $m$.  Translation covariance and Lorentz covariance should
be used to prove
\[
 \langle0|\Phi(x)|\mathbf p\rangle
 =\sqrt{\frac{Z}{(2\pi)^{D-1}\,2\omega_{\mathbf p}}}\,\ee^{+\ii p\mathbin{\cdot}x}.
\]
Define $Z$, discuss the harmless phase convention, and translate the result
to covariantly normalized states.  As a check, derive the associated
one-particle pole and its residue in the two-point function.
\end{exercise}
