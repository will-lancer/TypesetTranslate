\begin{exercise}[LSZ reduction for particles with spin]{I-CH03-016}
Extend the scalar LSZ construction to stable massive particles with spin.
Use the mostly-plus metric and one-particle normalization
\[
 \langle\mathbf q,\lambda'|\mathbf p,\lambda\rangle
 =\delta_{\lambda'\lambda}\delta^{D-1}(\mathbf q-\mathbf p).
\]
For a vector interpolating field, derive the vacuum-to-particle matrix element
in terms of polarization vectors, give their normalization and completeness
relation, and identify the tensor residue of the two-point pole.  Repeat the
analysis for a Dirac interpolating field using $u$ and $v$ spinors satisfying
$\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=-2\eta^{\mu\nu}$ with $\slashed p=\gamma^\mu p_\mu$.  State the
corresponding form for a symmetric transverse traceless integer-spin
polarization tensor and for a tensor--spinor of half-integer spin.  In
momentum space, give the inverse-propagator operation, the factor of
$Z^{-1/2}$, and the physical polarization or spinor attached to each external
leg.  Include incoming and outgoing fermions and anti-fermions, and state the
qualification needed for massless gauge particles.
\end{exercise}
