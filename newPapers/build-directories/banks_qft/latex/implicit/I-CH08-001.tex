\begin{exercise}[The general renormalizable gauge theory]{I-CH08-001}
Work in four space-time dimensions with a compact gauge group
$G=\prod_rG_r$, Hermitian generators, left-handed Weyl fermions $\psi_I$,
and scalar fields $\phi_i$ in arbitrary finite-dimensional unitary
representations.  Take $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-\ii A_\mu$ and
$[D_\mu,D_\nu]=-\ii F_{\mu\nu}$.  Use canonical dimensions and gauge
invariance to construct the most general local, Lorentz-invariant,
perturbatively renormalizable Lagrangian.  Display the Yang--Mills and theta
terms, both kinetic terms, the scalar potential, fermion masses, and Yukawa
couplings.  State the invariant-tensor conditions on every coefficient and
explain why Pauli couplings and four-fermion interactions are absent.  State
the additional anomaly-cancellation requirement for a consistent chiral
quantum gauge theory.
\end{exercise}
