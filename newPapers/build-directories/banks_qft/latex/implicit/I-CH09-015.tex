\begin{exercise}[Canceling the one-loop fermion pole]{I-CH09-015}
Banks finds, with $\epsilon=4-d_{\mathrm{reg}}$ and
$\alpha=e^2/(4\pi)$,
\[
  \Sigma^\infty(p)=-\frac{\alpha}{2\pi\epsilon}
  \left[\kappa(\slashed p-\ii m_0)+3\ii m_0\right].
\]
Insert
\[
  Z_2=1+\kappa\frac{\alpha}{2\pi\epsilon},
  \qquad
  \delta m=-3\frac{\alpha}{2\pi\epsilon}m_0
\]
into the renormalized inverse propagator and verify cancellation of both its
$\slashed p$ and mass poles through order $\alpha$.  Keep track of the
$O(\alpha^2)$ product that is consistently omitted.  Show that the mass
renormalization is multiplicative and gauge-parameter independent.  Check the
Landau-gauge and massless limits separately.
\end{exercise}
