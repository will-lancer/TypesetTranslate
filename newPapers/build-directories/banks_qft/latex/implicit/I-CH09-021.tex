\begin{exercise}[Wick rotation of the dimensional integral table]{I-CH09-021}
For $U>0$, first evaluate in the convergence domains
\[
  I_n^E(U)=\int\frac{\dd^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}p_E}{(2\pi)^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}}
  \frac{1}{(p_E^2+U)^n},
  \qquad
  I_{n,\mu\nu}^E(U)=\int\frac{\dd^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}p_E}{(2\pi)^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}}
  \frac{p_{E\mu}p_{E\nu}}{(p_E^2+U)^n}
\]
by Schwinger parameters.  Analytically continue the answers and perform the
Wick rotation with the Feynman prescription to derive Banks's two
Minkowski-space formulas
\[
  \int\frac{\dd^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}p}{(2\pi)^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}}
  \frac{1}{(p^2+U-\ii0)^n},
  \qquad
  \int\frac{\dd^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}p}{(2\pi)^{d_{\mathrm{reg}}}}
  \frac{p^\mu p^\nu}{(p^2+U-\ii0)^n}.
\]
Track every factor of $\ii$, $(-1)^n$, $2$, and $\pi$.  Explain why the
argument of the numerator gamma function fixes the power of $U$.  Check the
tensor formula by Lorentz contraction and dimensional analysis.
\end{exercise}
