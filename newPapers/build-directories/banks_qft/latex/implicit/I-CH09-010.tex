\begin{exercise}[One-loop QED with many charged fields]{I-CH09-010}
Consider an Abelian gauge field coupled to Dirac fields $\psi_f$ and complex
scalars $\phi_s$ of charges $q_f$ and $q_s$.  Include every gauge-invariant
scalar mass and quartic interaction allowed by the charges, and impose any
stated flavor symmetries explicitly.  Work in the covariant gauge
\[
  D_{\mu\nu}(k)=\frac{1}{k^2}
  \left(\delta_{\mu\nu}-(1-\kappa)\frac{k_\mu k_\nu}{k^2}\right),
  \qquad d_{\mathrm{reg}}=4-\epsilon.
\]

Evaluate the one-loop photon polarization from every species, including the
scalar seagull graph, and show that it is transverse.  Derive
\[
  b=\frac43\sum_fq_f^2+\frac13\sum_sq_s^2,
  \qquad
  Z_3=1-\frac{e^2b}{8\pi^2\epsilon}+O(e^4),
  \qquad
  \beta(e)=\frac{b e^3}{16\pi^2}+O(e^5).
\]
Generalize Banks's fermion self-energy to every $f$, compute the scalar
self-energy, and identify the vertex, mass, and quartic counterterms required
by the Ward identities.  Explain why the one-loop effective action remains a
linear combination of the local operators already allowed in the general
renormalizable Lagrangian.  Recover Banks's single unit-charge Dirac result
and check that one complex scalar contributes one quarter as much to $b$.
\end{exercise}
