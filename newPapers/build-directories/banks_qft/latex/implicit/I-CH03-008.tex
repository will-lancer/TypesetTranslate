\begin{exercise}[Normalization and vacuum-bubble cancellation]{I-CH03-008}
Write
\[
 I[J,g]=\int[d\phi]\,\ee^{\ii S_0[\phi]+\ii\int\dd^D x\,J(x)\phi(x)}
 \exp\!\left(-\ii g\int\dd^D x\,V(\phi(x))\right),
 \qquad Z[J,g]=\frac{I[J,g]}{I[0,g]},
\]
and let $I_0=I[0,0]$.  Expand numerator and denominator through $g^2$ and
show that every Gaussian moment occurs divided by $I_0$.  Prove to all orders
that the residual denominator removes exactly the vacuum components, leaving
the normalized free generator
\[
 Z_0[J]=\frac{I[J,0]}{I_0}
 =\exp\!\left[-\frac12\int\dd^D x\,\dd^D y\,
 J(x)D_F(x-y)J(y)\right]
\]
as the source of Wick contractions.  Check the identity $Z[0,g]=1$.
\end{exercise}
