\begin{exercise}[Large-mass counting in the worldline action]{I-CH06-004}
In the worldline expression~\eqref{eq:6.15}, set
\(u=sM_\mu^2\) and \(v=\tau M_\mu^2\).  Rewrite separately the proper-time
mass phase, the free kinetic action, the line integral of \(A_\mu\), and
the spin coupling to \(F_{\mu\nu}\).  For fixed endpoints and background
fields independent of \(M_\mu\), determine the power of \(M_\mu\) carried
by each term.  Explain why the free trajectory supplies the leading saddle
and identify the relative suppression of the background-field terms.
\end{exercise}
