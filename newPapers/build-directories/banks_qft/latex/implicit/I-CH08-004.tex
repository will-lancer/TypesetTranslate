\begin{exercise}[BRST cohomology and the physical sector]{I-CH08-004}
Let the nilpotent BRST charge $Q$ have ghost number one.  Define
$Z=\ker Q$, $B=\operatorname{im}Q$, and $H_Q=Z/B$.
Show that $B\subset Z$, that an exact state represents the zero class, and
that a representative of a nonzero physical class cannot be exact.  Next,
in perturbation theory, organize the longitudinal and time-like gauge modes,
would-be Goldstone modes, ghosts, and antighosts into BRST quartets.  Use the
oscillator contracting homotopy to identify the ghost-number-zero asymptotic
state cohomology.  Separately, construct the doublet homotopy for local
polynomials and show that their ghost-number-zero cohomology has
gauge-invariant representatives, modulo equations of motion and total
derivatives.  State the assumptions behind each result and connect the local
one with gauge-fixing independence of physical correlators.
\end{exercise}
