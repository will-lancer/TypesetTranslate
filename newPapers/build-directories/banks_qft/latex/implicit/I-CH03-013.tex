\begin{exercise}[A Feynman-graph setup drill]{I-CH03-013}
In Lorentzian $\lambda\phi^4$ theory with
\(\mathcal L_I=-\lambda\phi^4/4!\), set up the following connected Green-function
contributions without evaluating any loop integral:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
 \item the order-$\lambda$ two-point tadpole;
 \item the order-$\lambda$ connected four-point contact graph;
 \item the order-$\lambda^2$ two-point sunset graph with three lines joining
 the two vertices;
 \item one channel of the order-$\lambda^2$ four-point bubble graph.
\end{enumerate}
For each graph give its position-space integral, vertex and propagator factors,
external legs, internal lines, loop number, and symmetry factor.  For the
bubble, also choose a loop momentum and write the amputated momentum-space
integral.  Relate one direct count to the quartic examples in Appendix~D and
state the superficial ultraviolet behavior of the loop examples in four
dimensions.
\end{exercise}
