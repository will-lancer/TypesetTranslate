\begin{exercise}[Higher Dirac traces and contractions]{I-APPC-002}
Work in four dimensions ($D=4$) with
\[
 \{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=-2\eta^{\mu\nu},
 \qquad \operatorname{tr}\mathbf 1_4=4,
 \qquad \gamma_5=\ii\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3,
\]
using $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,+1,+1,+1)$ and
$\epsilon^{0123}=+1$.  Introduce
\begin{align*}
 T_m{}^{\mu_1\ldots\mu_m}
   &=\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^{\mu_1}\cdots\gamma^{\mu_m}),\\
 T_{m,5}{}^{\mu_1\ldots\mu_m}
   &=\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^{\mu_1}\cdots\gamma^{\mu_m}\gamma_5),\\
 K_k(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_k)
   &=\gamma^\lambda\gamma_{\mu_1}\cdots\gamma_{\mu_k}\gamma_\lambda.
\end{align*}

Derive a recurrence for $T_{2n}$ from the Clifford relation and cyclicity
of the trace.  Use it to reduce $T_6$ explicitly to products of three
metrics, and give the complete compact reduction of $T_8$ in terms of
$T_6$.  State the corresponding all-orders pairing rule.

Next show that odd $T_{m,5}$ vanish, establish
\[
 T_{4,5}{}^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=-4\ii\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma},
\]
and reduce $T_{6,5}$ explicitly to products of the metric and the
Levi-Civita tensor.  Give an
all-orders rule that also supplies the full $T_{8,5}$ reduction.

Finally derive an induction relation for $K_k$.  Use it first to test the
free indices and gamma-matrix order in Banks's displayed $K_3$ and $K_4$
lines, recording any required correction.  Then continue the list by
computing $K_5$ and $K_6$, including the related contractions with a
$\gamma_5$ immediately before the final $\gamma_\lambda$.  Prove the useful
antisymmetric-product identity
\[
 \gamma^\lambda\gamma_{\mu_1\ldots\mu_r}\gamma_\lambda
 =(-1)^{r+1}(D-2r)\gamma_{\mu_1\ldots\mu_r},
 \qquad
 \gamma_{\mu_1\ldots\mu_r}
 =\frac1{r!}\sum_{\pi\in S_r}\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)
 \gamma_{\mu_{\pi(1)}}\cdots\gamma_{\mu_{\pi(r)}},
\]
and use it to check the higher-order contractions.  Its $D=4$ specialization
is the one used by the $4\times4$ matrices above. Reduce every trace to
$\eta$ and $\epsilon$, with all signs fixed by the conventions above.
\end{exercise}
