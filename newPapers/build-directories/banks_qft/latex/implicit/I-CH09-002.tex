\begin{exercise}[Dilatations at an RG fixed point]{I-CH09-002}
Let $S_*$ be a local action at a fixed point of the Wilsonian RG in $D$
space-time dimensions.  Perform one RG step consisting of a change of cutoff
$\Lambda\mapsto\ee^{-t}\Lambda$, followed by $x\mapsto\ee^t x$ and field
rescalings that restore the cutoff.  Show that fixed-point invariance makes
$S_*$ invariant under this space-time dilatation.

For a scaling operator $O_I$ with dimension $\Delta_I$, determine the scaling
of a perturbation
\[
  \delta S=g^I\int\dd^D x\,O_I(x)
\]
and derive its linearized RG eigenvalue $y_I=D-\Delta_I$.  Classify the
perturbation as relevant, marginal, or irrelevant.  For a field $\Phi$ of
dimension $\Delta_\Phi$, derive the position-space scaling law for its
$n$-point function.  Use the free massless scalar as a check, including the
dimensions of $\phi^n$.
\end{exercise}
