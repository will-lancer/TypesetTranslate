\begin{exercise}[Termwise finiteness of the smooth-cutoff expansion]{I-CH09-006}
Consider Banks's Euclidean scalar action with quadratic kernel
$K(p^2/\Lambda^2)$ and smooth momentum-space vertices
$g_n(p_1,\ldots,p_n;\Lambda)$.  Assume that $K(p^2/\Lambda^2)$ agrees with
$p^2+m^2$ at low momentum, grows at least exponentially for
$|p|\gg\Lambda$, and that every $g_n$ is bounded by a polynomial in its
momenta.

For a fixed Feynman graph, choose independent loop momenta and prove that its
integrand is bounded by a polynomial times a decaying Gaussian.  Conclude
that the loop integral and each of its ultraviolet subintegrals converge.
Explain why this establishes finiteness coefficient by coefficient in the
perturbation series.  Treat the possible massless infrared singularity by
moving a quadratic mass term from $S_I$ into $S_0$, and check that this change
leaves the ultraviolet estimate intact.
\end{exercise}
