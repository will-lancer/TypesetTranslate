\begin{exercise}[Testing massless-baryon anomaly matching]{I-CH08-018}
Consider parity-symmetric $SU(N)$ gauge theory with $N_F$ massless Dirac
fundamentals and assume confinement.  For $N=N_F=3$, decompose the
three-quark flavor representations and use
$T=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2)$ to compare the ultraviolet
$SU(3)_L^3$ anomaly with that of a massless baryon in the symmetric
$\mathbf{10}$.  Show that anomaly matching would require a fractional baryon
multiplicity.  Explain the immediate obstruction when $N$ is even.

For odd $N$, give one flavor a positive mass.  Apply the
gauge-background-independent massive-propagator bound, together with the
persistent-mass assumption for the full composite correlator, to exclude a
massless pole from every baryon containing the massive flavor.  Derive the
resulting flavor-decoupling condition on any proposed integer set of
massless-baryon multiplicities.  Formulate its combination with the cubic and
mixed anomaly equations under successive $N_F\mapsto N_F-1$ restrictions.
Explain why a fixed-$(N,N_F)$ algebraic solution does not suffice.  Treat
Banks's all-$(N,N_F)$ exclusion as conditional on the required integer
restriction-matrix result and on the stated infrared assumptions.
\end{exercise}
