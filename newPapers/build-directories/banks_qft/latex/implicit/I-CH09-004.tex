\begin{exercise}[Why finite systems have analytic free energy]{I-CH09-004}
Let a finite system have partition function
\[
  Z_N(\beta,g)=\operatorname{Tr}\exp[-\beta H_N(g)],
  \qquad
  F_N=-\frac{1}{\beta V_N}\log Z_N,
\]
where the Hilbert space is finite-dimensional and the entries of $H_N(g)$ are
analytic in the real control parameters $g$.  Prove that $Z_N$ is analytic in
$\beta$ and $g$, that it is positive on the physical real domain, and that
$F_N$ is real analytic there.  Extend the proof to a finite-dimensional
classical integral under a uniform integrable bound.

Describe precisely how the thermodynamic limit $V_N\to\infty$ can lose this
property.  Give the equivalent descriptions in terms of nonuniform
convergence of derivatives and accumulation of complex zeros of $Z_N$ onto
the real parameter axis.  Check the finite-system statement for one Ising
spin in a magnetic field.
\end{exercise}
