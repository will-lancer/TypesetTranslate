\begin{exercise}[Strong-coupling Wilson-loop area law]{I-CH08-007}
In the integer-plaquette representation of a $\mathbb Z_N$ lattice gauge
theory, take the weight
$\exp[-g^2\sum_p l_p^2]$.  Insertion of a charge-one Wilson loop $C$ changes
the constraint to $\Delta_\nu l_{\mu\nu}=w_\mu(C)$, while the vacuum
constraint has zero right-hand side.  Show that every numerator configuration
is an integer two-chain whose boundary is $C$.  Prove that, at large $g^2$,
the leading configuration is supported on a surface of least lattice area.
Derive the leading Wilson-loop expectation value, estimate the first
strong-coupling corrections, and extract the static potential from a large
rectangular loop.  Restore the lattice spacing and identify the string
tension.
\end{exercise}
