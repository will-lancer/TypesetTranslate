\begin{exercise}[What analyticity of the spectral function means]{I-CH03-007}
Make precise the statement following Eq.~\eqref{eq:3.23} about perturbative
spectral functions.  Begin with a spatially smeared free scalar field and
compute its two-point spectral measure.  Determine its delta-function or
threshold structure, its large-energy behavior for a Schwartz smearing
function, and the analyticity domain of its complex-time transform.  Next use
the Cutkosky phase-space form of a fixed-order perturbative contribution to
identify the corresponding thresholds and large-energy bound.  State the
distributional, smeared version of the claim that is valid order by order, and
show that it supplies the convergence needed for Euclidean-time continuation.
\end{exercise}
