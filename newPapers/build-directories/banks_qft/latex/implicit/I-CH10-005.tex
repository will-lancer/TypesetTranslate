\begin{exercise}[Normalization of the instanton zero mode]{I-CH10-005}
Let $x_c(t)$ be an isolated Euclidean instanton between degenerate minima whose
vacuum energy has been set to zero.  Define
\[
  S_0=\int\dd t\left[\frac12\dot x_c^{\,2}+V(x_c)\right],
  \qquad
  \delta_0(t)=\frac{\dot x_c(t)}{\sqrt{S_0}}.
\]
Using the instanton equation and its first integral
$\dot x_c^{\,2}=2V(x_c)$, verify both
\[
  \left[-\frac{\dd^2}{\dd t^2}+V''(x_c)\right]\delta_0=0,
  \qquad
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\dd t\,\delta_0^2=1.
\]
State why the surface terms and the normalization integral are well defined.
\end{exercise}
