\begin{exercise}[Gaussian determinant and its phase]{I-CH03-004}
Let $K$ be a complex symmetric $n\times n$ matrix whose real part is positive
definite, and use ordinary Lebesgue measure in
\[
 I(J)=\int_{\mathbb R^n}\dd^ny\,
 \exp\!\left(-\frac12y^iK_{ij}y^j+\ii y^iJ_i\right).
\]
Complete the square, justify the contour shift, and compute both $I(J)$ and
$I(J)/I(0)$.  Fix the square-root branch of $\det K$ by continuous
continuation from real positive-definite matrices.  Then continue to an
oscillatory quadratic form using the $\ii\varepsilon$ prescription.  Check the
answer for $n=1$, $J=0$, and compare the resulting normalization with
Eq.~\eqref{eq:3.16}.
\end{exercise}
