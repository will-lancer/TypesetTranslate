\begin{exercise}[On-shell electromagnetic form factors]{I-CH06-005}
Let \(p'=p+q\), with \(p^2=p'^2=-m^2\), and consider the parity-even
on-shell matrix element \(\bar u(p')\Gamma_\mu(p,q)u(p)\).  Begin with the
complete basis of Dirac matrices and all available vectors.  Use parity,
the two Dirac equations, \(\bar u(p')\slashed q\,u(p)=0\), and the Gordon
identity to prove that the matrix element can be written as
\[
 \bar u(p')\left[
 F_1(q^2,m^2)\gamma_\mu
 +F_2(q^2,m^2)\frac{\gamma_{\mu\nu}q^\nu}{2m}
 +F_3(q^2,m^2)q_\mu\right]u(p).
\]
Use Banks's convention
\(\gamma^{\mu\nu}=\tfrac12(\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu-
\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)\), and show explicitly why no other parity-even
covariant remains independent.
\end{exercise}
