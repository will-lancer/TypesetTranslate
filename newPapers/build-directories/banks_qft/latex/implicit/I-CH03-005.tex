\begin{exercise}[Operator derivation of the Feynman propagator]{I-CH03-005}
Use
\[
 \phi(x)=\int\frac{\dd^{D-1}\mathbf p}{\sqrt{(2\pi)^{D-1}\,2\omega_{\mathbf p}}}
 \left[a(\mathbf p)\ee^{+\ii p\mathbin{\cdot}x}+a^\dagger(\mathbf p)\ee^{-\ii p\mathbin{\cdot}x}\right],
 \qquad [a(\mathbf p),a^\dagger(\mathbf q)]=\delta^{D-1}(\mathbf p-\mathbf q).
\]
Compute
\(\langle0|T\phi(x)\phi(y)|0\rangle\) separately for $x^0>y^0$ and
$x^0<y^0$.  Convert the result into a $p^0$ contour integral and prove
\[
 \langle0|T\phi(x)\phi(y)|0\rangle
 =\int\frac{\dd^D p}{(2\pi)^D}\,
 \frac{-\ii\ee^{+\ii p\mathbin{\cdot}(x-y)}}{p^2+m^2-\ii\varepsilon}.
\]
Locate both poles and match the contour selected in each time ordering.
Finally verify the Klein--Gordon Green-function equation and its equal-time
derivative jump.  State how these checks fix the same boundary condition as
the $\ii\varepsilon$ prescription in the functional integral.
\end{exercise}
