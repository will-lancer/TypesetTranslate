\begin{exercise}[Gradient RG flow and its fixed-point limit]{I-CH09-001}
Let $g^i(t)$ be dimensionless couplings on a finite-dimensional manifold
$\mathcal M$.  Suppose the class of RG flows under consideration admits a
real function $C(g)$ and a positive-definite metric $G_{ij}(g)$ for which the
local RG consistency conditions give
\[
  \partial_i C=-G_{ij}\beta^j,
  \qquad \dot g^i=\beta^i(g).
\]
Show that this is a gradient flow and that $C$ is a strict Lyapunov function.
Assume that a forward trajectory remains in a compact subset of $\mathcal M$
and that the stationary points in its limit set are isolated.  Prove that the
trajectory approaches an RG fixed point.  Linearize about that point and
relate the stable and unstable directions to irrelevant and relevant
couplings, with the orientation of RG time stated explicitly.  Check the
argument for one coupling with $G=1$.
\end{exercise}
