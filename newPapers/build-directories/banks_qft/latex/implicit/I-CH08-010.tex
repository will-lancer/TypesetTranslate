\begin{exercise}[The Vafa--Witten propagator bound]{I-CH08-010}
Work in four-dimensional Euclidean signature at $\theta_{\rm QCD}=0$ with equal quark mass
$m>0$.  First obtain the large-distance exponential bound for the free Dirac
propagator and explain, with the resolvent identity, why a rapidly decreasing
background preserves it.  For a gauge-background-independent argument, put
the theory on a local lattice regulator with unitary link variables.  Conjugate
the massive Dirac operator by an exponential position weight and use a
resolvent estimate to prove
\[
 \lVert S_A(x,y)\rVert\le C_m\ee^{-\mu|x-y|},
 \qquad 0<\mu<m/c_0,
\]
where $C_m,c_0$ do not depend on the gauge configuration.  Apply the bound to
the connected flavor-nonsinglet vector-current integrand and then to its
positive gauge-field average.
Show that the Fourier-transformed two-point function is analytic near zero
momentum and therefore has no Nambu--Goldstone pole.  List the roles of
$m>0$, positivity, locality, and the regulator.
\end{exercise}
