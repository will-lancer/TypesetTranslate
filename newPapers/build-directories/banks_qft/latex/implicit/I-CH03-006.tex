\begin{exercise}[Spectral continuation to Euclidean time]{I-CH03-006}
Suppose $t_1>\cdots>t_n$, the vacuum energy is zero, and the Hamiltonian is
bounded below.  Insert complete energy eigenstate sets into the ordered
$n$-point function and derive
\[
 G_n=\int\dd\rho(E_1,\ldots,E_{n-1})
 \exp\!\left[-\ii\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}E_k(t_k-t_{k+1})\right],
\]
including the matrix-element weights in $\dd\rho$.  Continue every
difference $t_k-t_{k+1}$ to a complex variable with negative imaginary part.
Give a growth condition on the spectral measure that permits differentiation
under the integral and proves analyticity in this tube.  Take
$t_k-t_{k+1}=-\ii\tau_k$, $\tau_k>0$, and explain how the original operator
ordering is retained.  For the free two-point function, perform the matching
$p^0$ contour rotation and recover Eq.~\eqref{eq:3.21}, including the condition
that the large contour arc makes no contribution.
\end{exercise}
