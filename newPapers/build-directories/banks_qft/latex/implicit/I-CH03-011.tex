\begin{exercise}[Symmetry factors from orbit--stabilizer]{I-CH03-011}
Consider real scalar interactions
\(\mathcal L_I=-\sum_k\lambda_k\phi^k/k!\).  For a fixed graph $G$, label
every interaction vertex and every field slot at a vertex.  Let
$\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ preserve incidence, vertex valence, and every labeled
external leg; include permutations of parallel edges and flips of self-loops.
Use the orbit--stabilizer theorem to prove that the Wick-contraction
coefficient left after the factors $1/n_k!$ and $(1/k!)^{n_k}$ is
\[
 S_G=\frac{1}{|\operatorname{Aut}(G)|}.
\]
Match this result to direct contraction counts for a one-vertex two-point
tadpole and for two quartic vertices joined by four lines, the latter being
one of the examples in Appendix~D.
\end{exercise}
