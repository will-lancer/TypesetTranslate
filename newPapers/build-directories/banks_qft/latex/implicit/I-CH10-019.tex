\begin{exercise}[The boundary map from monopole classes to loops in the unbroken group]{I-CH10-019}
Let a connected gauge group $G$ be broken to the stabilizer $H$ of a vacuum
$\phi_0$.  A finite-energy Higgs field at spatial infinity defines a based map
$S^2\to G/H$.  Cover $S^2$ by two disks, lift the map to $G$ on each disk,
and let $h:S^1\to H$ be their transition function on the equator.  This
constructs the boundary map
\[
  \partial:\pi_2(G/H)\longrightarrow\pi_1(H).
\]
Show directly, using gluing or concatenation of representatives, that
$\partial$ is a group homomorphism.  Then use the long exact sequence of the
fibration $H\to G\to G/H$, together with $\pi_2(G)=0$ for a Lie group, to
prove
\[
  \pi_2(G/H)\simeq
  \ker\!\left[\pi_1(H)\longrightarrow\pi_1(G)\right].
\]
Deduce Banks's stated isomorphism $\pi_2(G/H)\simeq\pi_1(H)$ when $G$ is
simply connected, and describe what a class in the kernel means for the
asymptotic gauge field of a monopole.
\end{exercise}
