\begin{exercise}[The bounce path through a multi-field target space]{I-CH10-007}
For canonically normalized scalar fields $\phi^i$ and an $O(D)$-invariant
Euclidean bounce, start from
\[
  S_E=\Omega_{D-1}\int_0^\infty\dd r\,r^{D-1}
  \left[\frac12\phi_r^i\phi_r^i+V(\phi)\right].
\]
Write the field trajectory as $\phi^i(r)=\Phi^i(\ell(r))$, where $\ell$ is arc
length in field space and $T^i=\dd\Phi^i/\dd\ell$ has unit norm.  Derive the
vector bounce equation, then project it along and perpendicular to $T^i$.
Show that the tangential equation is a one-field bounce equation for the path
length with effective potential $V(\Phi(\ell))$, while the normal equation
determines the curvature of the path through field space.  Give the regularity
and false-vacuum boundary conditions.  Briefly state the covariant replacement
when the kinetic term uses a field-space metric $G_{ij}(\phi)$.
\end{exercise}
