\begin{exercise}[Pole equations and RG functions in MS]{I-CH09-017}
Let $g$ be a dimensionless four-dimensional coupling and write, in
$d_{\mathrm{reg}}=4-\epsilon$ dimensions,
\[
  g_0=\mu^{r\epsilon}F(g,\epsilon),
  \qquad
  F(g,\epsilon)=g+\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{a_n(g)}{\epsilon^n}.
\]
Use the forest structure of subdivergences to show that an $L$-loop
renormalization factor can have poles only through $1/\epsilon^L$.  Apply
$\mu\dd g_0/\dd\mu=0$ and derive the recursion relations that cancel all
higher poles.  For $r=1$, prove that the four-dimensional beta function is
\[
  \beta_4(g)=g a_1'(g)-a_1(g).
\]

If
$\ln Z_\Phi=\sum_{n\geq1}z_n(g)/\epsilon^n$ and
$\gamma_\Phi=\tfrac12\mu\dd\ln Z_\Phi/\dd\mu$, derive $\gamma_\Phi$ from
$z_1$ and show explicitly how the higher-pole terms cancel.  Generalize the
formula to several couplings.  Check the one- and two-loop pole orders.
\end{exercise}
