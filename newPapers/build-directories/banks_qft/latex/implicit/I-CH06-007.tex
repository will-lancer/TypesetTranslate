\begin{exercise}[Schwinger's one-loop magnetic form factor]{I-CH06-007}
Set \(q^2=0\) in the Feynman-parameter integral of
equation~\eqref{eq:6.18}.  Evaluate the integral over the simplex
\(x,y,z\geq0\), \(x+y+z=1\), keeping track of the apparent endpoint at
\(z=1\).  Match the coefficient of
\(+\gamma^{\mu\nu}q_\nu/(2m)\) to the form-factor convention of
Section~6.4 and prove
\[
 F_2(0)=\frac{e^2}{8\pi^2}=\frac{\alpha}{2\pi}.
\]
Explain briefly why taking the photon mass to zero is safe for this magnetic
form factor at this order.
\end{exercise}
