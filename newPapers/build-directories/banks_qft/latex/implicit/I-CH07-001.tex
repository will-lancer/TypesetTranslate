\begin{exercise}[Symmetry of the connected and 1PI functionals]{I-CH07-001}
Let a multiplet of fields transform linearly as
$\phi(x)\mapsto R\phi(x)$, where $R$ belongs to a continuous internal
symmetry group.  Assume that the action and functional measure are invariant
and that surface terms vanish.  With column sources and the pairing
$\int\dd^D x\,J^T\phi$, define
\[
 Z[J]=\int[\dd\phi]\,
 \exp\left\{\ii S[\phi]+\ii\int\dd^D x\,J^T\phi\right\},
 \qquad Z[J]=\ee^{\ii W[J]}.
\]
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item Show directly by a change of variables that $Z$ and $W$ are invariant
under the inverse source transformation $J\mapsto R^{-T}J$.

\item Prove that the classical field
$\varphi[J]=\delta W/\delta J$ transforms as $\varphi\mapsto R\varphi$ and
that the Legendre transform, in Banks's convention,
\[
 \Gamma[\varphi]=W[J]-\int\dd^D x\,J^T\varphi,
\]
is invariant under the original field transformation:
$\Gamma[R\varphi]=\Gamma[\varphi]$.

\item Give the corresponding infinitesimal functional identities and state
where an anomaly or a non-vanishing boundary term enters the argument.
\end{enumerate}
\end{exercise}
