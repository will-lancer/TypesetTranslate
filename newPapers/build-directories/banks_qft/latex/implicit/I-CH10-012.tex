\begin{exercise}[Magnetic charge as a surface flux]{I-CH10-012}
In the three-dimensional Euclidean $SU(2)$ Higgs model, the static
interpretation is in the $D=4$ Lorentzian monopole theory.  Define
\[
  \nu M=\frac12\int\dd^3\mathbf x\,
  \epsilon_{\mu\nu\lambda}F^a{}_{\mu\nu}(D_\lambda\phi)^a,
  \qquad
  B^a{}_\lambda=\frac12\epsilon_{\lambda\mu\nu}F^a{}_{\mu\nu}.
\]
Assume finite-action boundary conditions $|\phi|\to\nu$ and
$D_\mu\phi\to0$.  Apply the covariant Leibniz rule to
$\phi^aB^a{}_\lambda$, use the non-Abelian Bianchi identity
$D_\lambda B^a{}_\lambda=0$, and use the fact that the contracted expression is
a gauge singlet.  Derive the divergence formula and then prove
\[
  M=\frac1\nu\int_{S^2_\infty}\phi^aF^a,
  \qquad F^a=\frac12F^a{}_{\mu\nu}\dd x^\mu\wedge\dd x^\nu.
\]
Check the factors by translating between vector and differential-form notation.
\end{exercise}
