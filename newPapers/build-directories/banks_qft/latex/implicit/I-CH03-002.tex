\begin{exercise}[Time ordering inside the Schwinger--Dyson series]{I-CH03-002}
For
\[
 Z[J]=\langle0|T\exp\!\left(\ii\int\dd^D y\,J(y)\phi(y)\right)|0\rangle,
\]
use the Dyson-series definition of the time-ordered exponential to prove that
\[
 \frac{\delta Z}{\ii\delta J(x)}
 =\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\ii^n}{n!}
 \int\prod_{r=1}^n\dd^D y_r\,J(y_r)
 \langle0|T\phi(x)\phi(y_1)\cdots\phi(y_n)|0\rangle.
\]
In particular, write the $n=1$ term with explicit Heaviside functions and
explain why the distinguished field at $x$ must take part in the same time
ordering as every integrated field.  For a polynomial $V'(\phi)$, extend the
argument to several functional derivatives and obtain the time-ordered
insertion used on the right-hand side of Eq.~\eqref{eq:3.4}.
\end{exercise}
