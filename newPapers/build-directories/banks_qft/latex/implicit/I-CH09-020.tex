\begin{exercise}[Full one-loop gluon self-energy in covariant gauge]{I-CH09-020}
For a Yang--Mills theory with gauge group $G$, use
\[
  D_{\rho\sigma}{}^{ab}(k)=\frac{-\ii\delta^{ab}}{k^2-\ii0}
  \left[\eta_{\rho\sigma}-(1-\kappa)
  \frac{k_\rho k_\sigma}{k^2-\ii0}\right]
\]
and the corresponding Faddeev--Popov rules.  In $d_{\mathrm{reg}}=4-\epsilon$,
calculate the
$q^2\eta_{\mu\nu}$ and $q_\mu q_\nu$ pole coefficients of the triple-gluon
loop, the four-gluon tadpole, and the ghost loop separately.  Add Weyl-fermion
and complex-scalar loops in representations $R_F$ and $R_S$, including the
scalar seagull.

Show that the pure-gauge and ghost sum is
\[
  \Pi_{\mu\nu}{}^{ab}\big|_{\mathrm{div}}
  =\frac{\ii g^2C_2(G)\delta^{ab}}{16\pi^2\epsilon}
  \left(\frac{13}{3}-\kappa\right)
  (q^2\eta_{\mu\nu}-q_\mu q_\nu),
\]
before adding matter.  Verify that the longitudinal pole cancels only in the
sum and record the matter correction.  Explain why the massless tadpole
vanishes in dimensional regularization.  Check Feynman gauge against Banks's
trace calculation and distinguish the gauge-field wave-function coefficient
from the gauge-independent beta-function coefficient.
\end{exercise}
