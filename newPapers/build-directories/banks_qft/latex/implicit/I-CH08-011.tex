\begin{exercise}[Covariant and consistent anomalies]{I-CH08-011}
Use differential forms, $F=\dd A+A^2$, and suppress the common
representation-dependent normalization.  Starting from the consistent
four-dimensional anomaly obtained by descent from $\Tr F^3$, compare it with
the covariant expression proportional to $\Tr(\omega F^2)$.  Construct the
local Bardeen--Zumino polynomial $K_{\rm BZ}(A)$ and verify
\[
 J_{\rm cov}=J_{\rm cons}+K_{\rm BZ},\qquad
 \mathcal A_{\rm cov}-\mathcal A_{\rm cons}=D K_{\rm BZ}.
\]
Expand the identity through cubic order in $A$.  Explain in what precise sense
this is a local redefinition, why the consistent anomaly obeys the
Wess--Zumino condition, and why the covariant expression is useful despite
failing that condition as an effective-action variation.
\end{exercise}
