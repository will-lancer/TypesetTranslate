\begin{exercise}[Spin connection and hypercharge quantization]{I-CH08-014}
Work in four-dimensional Lorentzian space-time with
$\eta^{ab}=\operatorname{diag}(-,+,+,+)$ in the tangent frame.
Impose zero torsion on a vierbein $e_\mu{}^a$:
\[
 \partial_\mu e_\nu{}^a-\partial_\nu e_\mu{}^a
 =\omega_\mu{}^{ab}e_{\nu b}-\omega_\nu{}^{ab}e_{\mu b}.
\]
Solve algebraically for the antisymmetric spin connection
$\omega_\mu{}^{ab}$.  Under $e_\mu\mapsto\Lambda(x)e_\mu$, derive its
inhomogeneous local-Lorentz transformation law.

For one standard-model generation, impose cancellation of
$U(1)^3$, $U(1)SU(2)^2$, $U(1)SU(3)^2$, and mixed
$U(1)$--gravity anomalies:
\[
\begin{gathered}
6Y_q^3+3Y_{\bar u}^3+3Y_{\bar d}^3+2Y_l^3+Y_{\bar e}^3=0,\quad
3Y_q+Y_l=0,\quad 2Y_q+Y_{\bar u}+Y_{\bar d}=0,\\
6Y_q+3Y_{\bar u}+3Y_{\bar d}+2Y_l+Y_{\bar e}=0.
\end{gathered}
\]
Solve the system and show, on the nontrivial chiral branch, that every
hypercharge is an integer multiple of $Y_q$ up to the overall normalization
absorbed into the $U(1)$ coupling.  Classify the degenerate $Y_q=0$ branch
separately.
\end{exercise}
