\begin{exercise}[$SU(2)$ gauge bosons as charged vectors with $g=2$]{I-CH09-019}
Start from
\[
  \mathcal L_{\mathrm{YM}}=-\frac14F^a{}_{\mu\nu}F^{a\mu\nu},
  \qquad
  F^a{}_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A^a_\nu-\partial_\nu A^a_\mu
  +e\epsilon^{abc}A^b_\mu A^c_\nu.
\]
Define $A_\mu=A^3_\mu$ and
$W^\pm_\mu=(A^1_\mu\mp\ii A^2_\mu)/\sqrt2$.  Expand all three curvature
components and rewrite the Lagrangian in terms of the Abelian field strength,
the charged-vector kinetic term, its magnetic-moment interaction, and the
quartic $W$ interaction.

Compare the term bilinear in $W$ and linear in $F_{\mu\nu}$ with the most
general charged Proca interaction.  Derive the nonrelativistic magnetic
Hamiltonian and show that the Yang--Mills vector has gyromagnetic ratio
$g_{\mathrm{mag}}=2$.  Check the coefficient by expanding the square of
$F^3_{\mu\nu}$ and by taking the Abelian coupling to zero.
\end{exercise}
