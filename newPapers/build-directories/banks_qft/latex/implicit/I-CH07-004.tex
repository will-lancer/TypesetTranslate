\begin{exercise}[Two-flavor chiral dynamics]{I-CH07-004}
Consider QCD with two massless quarks in $D=4$,
$q=(u,d)^T$, and omit the anomalous singlet axial symmetry.  Write
$q_{L,R}=P_{L,R}q$ with $P_{L,R}=(1\mp\gamma_5)/2$, and use
$\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=-2\eta^{\mu\nu}$,
$\gamma_5=\ii\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3$, and
$\slashed p=\gamma^\mu p_\mu$.  Take $t^a=\tau^a/2$ with
$\Tr(t^at^b)=\delta^{ab}/2$, and suppose that the flavor matrix
$\langle\bar q_Lq_R\rangle$ is proportional to the identity.  Use Banks's
orientation for the pion field.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item Identify the exact non-abelian chiral group $G$, its unbroken subgroup
$H$, and the coset $G/H$.  Represent the three Nambu--Goldstone fields by an
$SU(2)$-valued field $\Sigma(x)$ and derive Banks's transformation law
$\Sigma\mapsto L^\dagger\Sigma R$.

\item Show that the unique parity-even two-derivative Lagrangian is, up to its
normalization,
\[
 \mathcal L_2=-f^2\Tr(\partial_\mu\Sigma^\dagger
 \partial^\mu\Sigma),
 \qquad
 \Sigma=\exp\left(\frac{\ii\pi^at^a}{f}\right).
\]
Expand $\mathcal L_2$ through fourth order in the pion fields.

\item Derive the left- and right-handed Noether currents and their leading
vector and axial parts.  Use the four-pion interaction to obtain the complete
tree-level $\pi\pi$ amplitude in the chiral limit and verify its soft limit.

\item Derive the vacuum-to-pion current matrix element.  Explain how it fixes
the leading weak pion decay amplitude and the current-algebra soft-pion
relation.  Keep track of the factor between Banks's parameter $f$ and a decay
constant defined with the full axial current.
\end{enumerate}
\end{exercise}
