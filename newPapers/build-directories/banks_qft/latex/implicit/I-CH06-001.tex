\begin{exercise}[The electron-mass term in pair production]{I-CH06-001}
For the process
\(e^-(p_-)+e^+(p_+)\to\mu^-(k_-)+\mu^+(k_+)\), retain the electron
mass in the two traces of equation~\eqref{eq:6.7}.  Starting from
\[
 \operatorname{tr}\!\left[(\slashed p_+-m_e)\gamma^\lambda
 (\slashed p_-+m_e)\gamma^\kappa\right]
 \operatorname{tr}\!\left[(\slashed k_-+m_\mu)\gamma_\lambda
 (\slashed k_+-m_\mu)\gamma_\kappa\right],
\]
find the complete term proportional to \(m_e^2\) that was omitted from
equation~\eqref{eq:6.8}.  Express it first through scalar products and then
in terms of \(q^2=(k_-+k_+)^2\).  Check its suppression in the
center-of-mass frame when \(E\gg m_\mu,m_e\).
\end{exercise}
