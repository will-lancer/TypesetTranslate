\begin{exercise}[Gauge-independent scattering in unitary and $R_\kappa$ gauges]{I-CH09-003}
Consider a spontaneously broken gauge theory quantized in an $R_\kappa$
gauge.  Write its gauge-fixed action as
\[
  S_\kappa=S_{\mathrm{inv}}+s\Psi_\kappa,
\]
where $s$ is the nilpotent BRST differential and the functional measure has no
BRST anomaly.  External asymptotic states are assumed to lie in the BRST
cohomology.

Differentiate a physical LSZ amplitude with respect to $\kappa$ and prove that
it vanishes.  Explain how the $\kappa\to\infty$ limit imposes unitary gauge and
therefore gives the same physical $S$-matrix as any finite renormalizable
$R_\kappa$ gauge.  Then compare the large-momentum behavior of the massive
vector propagator in the two gauges and explain why off-shell unitary-gauge
Green functions can require infinitely many subtractions.  Include a
tree-level check using exchange between conserved external currents.
\end{exercise}
