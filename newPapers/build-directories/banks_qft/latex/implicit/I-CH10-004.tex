\begin{exercise}[Dilute instantons in a symmetric double well]{I-CH10-004}
Consider a reflection-symmetric double-well potential with perturbative vacua
$\lvert L\rangle$ and $\lvert R\rangle$.  Let
\[
  \kappa=D^{-1/2}\ee^{-S_0/g^2}
\]
denote the complete one-instanton fugacity per unit Euclidean time, with the
zero-mode measure included in $D^{-1/2}$.  An instanton changes $L$ to $R$ and
an anti-instanton changes $R$ to $L$.  Evaluate the dilute-gas sums for
$\langle L\rvert\ee^{-\beta H}\lvert L\rangle$ and
$\langle R\rvert\ee^{-\beta H}\lvert L\rangle$.  Enforce alternation of the
events and equality of instanton and anti-instanton numbers for the diagonal
amplitude.  Obtain the trace, identify the even and odd energy eigenstates,
and derive their leading energy splitting.  Include the ordered collective-
coordinate integral that supplies the factorial.
\end{exercise}
