\begin{exercise}[Exponential tail of the Nielsen--Olesen flux tube]{I-CH08-006}
For the BPS flux-tube profile used in the monopole-confinement discussion,
\[
 f'(r)=\frac{a(r)}r f(r),\qquad
 a'(r)=r\bigl(f(r)^2-\phi_0^2\bigr),\qquad
 f(\infty)=\phi_0,\quad a(\infty)=0,
\]
linearize about the large-$r$ vacuum.  Derive a second-order equation for
$f-\phi_0$, select the solution compatible with the boundary conditions,
and find the leading asymptotic forms of both $f-\phi_0$ and $a$.  Use the
second BPS equation to show that the magnetic field has the same exponential
localization, and identify the length scale of the tail.
\end{exercise}
