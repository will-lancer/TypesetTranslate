\begin{exercise}[CP invariance and a real Yukawa basis]{I-CH05-003}
In $D=4$, let $\psi_i$ be left-handed Weyl fields with canonical kinetic terms, and let
$\phi_A$ denote complex scalar components.  Write the Yukawa interaction as
\begin{equation*}
  \mathcal L_Y=-\frac12\left(
    Y^A_{ij}\phi_A\epsilon_{ab}\psi_i^a\psi_j^b
    +\mathrm{h.c.}\right),
  \qquad (Y^A)^T=Y^A.
\end{equation*}
Basis changes may mix fields carrying the same gauge quantum numbers.  Assume
that the gauge couplings admit CP and that any internal symmetry contained in
$(CP)^2$ has been absorbed into the definition of the CP transformation.  Its
action on the internal labels can then be written
\begin{align*}
  \psi_i^a(x)&\longmapsto
    X_{ij}(\ii\sigma_2)^{a\dot a}
    \psi_{j\dot a}^\dagger(x_P),\qquad x_P=(t,-\mathbf x),\\
  \phi_A(x)&\longmapsto Z_{AB}\phi_B^*(x_P),
\end{align*}
where $X$ and $Z$ are unitary and
$XX^*=ZZ^*=1$.  Choose the fixed spinor phase so that the Weyl scalar
$\epsilon_{ab}\psi_i^a\psi_j^b$ maps to its Hermitian conjugate with the
flavor factors $X_{ik}X_{jl}$.

Derive the condition imposed on the collection of matrices $Y^A$.  Prove that
the interaction is CP-invariant exactly when compatible field bases exist in
which every Yukawa coefficient is real.  State how the conclusion is modified
when CP-odd pure-gauge terms are present, as in Banks's following discussion.
\end{exercise}
