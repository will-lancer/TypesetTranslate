## Fidelity notes

- The printed Contents text is not duplicated in a front-matter file because the
  reading edition generates its table of contents from the master document.
- The title and copyright material preserves the printed capitalization,
  affiliations, ISBN, cataloging data, copyright line, paper standard, and
  printer sequence.
- The Preface dagger footnote is attached to ``treatise'' on printed page vii.
  The Introduction dagger footnote is attached to ``Schweber's book'' on
  printed page 2.
- No state vectors, inner products, or other state expressions occur on these
  pages, so the Weinberg Dirac-state macros have no front-matter application.
- The repeated title leaves on PDF pages 003 and 011 are represented by the
  single native JHEP title treatment. The page-006 title line remains in the
  CIP block because it is source content on that page.
