## Notation decisions

The source scalar product is linear in its second argument.  It is rendered
as `\braket{\Phi}{\Psi}` throughout, with state vectors written in Dirac
notation.  The source's operator adjoint notation $A^*$ is rendered as
`A^\dagger`; the source star in this packet denotes the adjoint, not complex
conjugation.  The source's identity representation in $U(a,1)$ is rendered as
`U(a,\mathbf{1})`.  The spectral measure, Fourier transform, and Lorentz
cone retain their source roles; $\mathcal{H}$ is used for the Hilbert space.

The equation tags are retained as (2-111)--(2-114).  The direct-sum display
uses round delimiters around the two schematic alternatives, matching the
printed page.
