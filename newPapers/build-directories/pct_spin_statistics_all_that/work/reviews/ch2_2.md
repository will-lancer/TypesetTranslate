## Notation audit

- The source's `\mathscr{F}`, `\overline{\mathscr{F}}`, `\mathscr{S}`, and
  `\mathscr{S}'` are rendered as `\mathcal{F}`, `\overline{\mathcal{F}}`,
  `\mathcal{S}`, and `\mathcal{S}'` in the house style.
- The source metric statement has `g^{00}=+1=-g^{jj}`. The transcription
  uses the required mostly-plus convention
  `\eta^{00}=-1=-\eta^{jj}` with off-diagonal entries zero, and writes the
  Minkowski product with `\eta^{\mu\nu}`. This is a deliberate notation map,
  not a correction to the source's mathematical argument.
- The Fourier phases remain paired as `e^{-ip\cdot x}` and
  `e^{+ip\cdot x}`. Since the mostly-plus scalar product reverses the sign
  of the source's mostly-minus component expression when expressed with
  contravariant coordinates, later chapter-wide use of component phases must
  be checked against this definition.
- `|x|^2`, `|r|^2`, and `|p-q|^2` in the Gaussian proof are Euclidean norms
  associated with the Schwartz-space estimates. They are not replaced by
  the Lorentzian quadratic form.
- The source identity `1` in (2-38) and (2-48) is rendered as `\mathbf{1}`
  under the house identity convention.
- The source uses multiindex notation `r`, `s`, `t`, `k`, and `D^r`, `D^s`,
  `D^k`; these are retained. In particular, the `D^r[(ix)^s f(x)]` term
  and the bounding `D^r(x^s f(x))` term in (2-37) were checked against the
  page image.
