## Notation and fidelity notes

- Source Hilbert-space parentheses were rendered in Dirac form. Vacuum states
  remain \(\ket{\Psi_0}\) and matrix elements use bra-kets.
- The source metric is used: \(a^2=-1\) is spacelike in the convention
  \(\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,+1,+1,+1)\). Four-vector integrations are
  written as \(\dd^4x\), with the source's suppressed contractions made
  explicit as \(p\mathbin{\cdot}x\).
- Stars in (3-33) and the corresponding proof are hermitian adjoints and
  were rendered as \({}^\dagger\). Stars in (3-38)--(3-39) are the source's
  charge-conjugation/conjugation symbols and remain \({}^*\), as the source
  explicitly distinguishes (3-38) from the hermitian-conjugation rule.
- The dotted spinor indices in (3-38) were transcribed as
  \((\dot\alpha),(\dot\beta),(\dot\mu),(\dot\nu)\). The scan is clear.
- The source's line after (3-24) has a conjugated first factor,
  \(\overline{(f_n-f_m)}(f_n-f_m)\); this is represented with the two
  arguments shown explicitly in the native integral.
- The source prints the spectral-support relation with
  \(\xi_j+a\) and \(j=1,2,\ldots,n\), although the displayed relative
  coordinate list ends at \(\xi_{n-1}\). The printed range and expression are
  retained rather than silently repairing the indexing.
- The source prints \(j=1,\ldots,n\) in the definition
  \(\zeta_j=\xi_j-i\eta_j\) while the tube has \(n-1\) relative coordinates.
  That range is retained in the theorem statement.
- The source prints the estimate \(R_0=\frac18[|a^0|+|a|]^{-1}\lambda\).
  The native form writes the spatial magnitude as \(|\mathbf a|\), matching
  the Euclidean \(R\)-norm used in the surrounding display.
- The free-field scalar-product line has \(\widetilde f(p)\widetilde g(p)\)
  without a displayed complex-conjugation bar in the scan. That reading is
  retained.
- The final source line ends with “once we have computed the domain of
  holomorphy explicitly, we can”. The native file ends at the same point.
