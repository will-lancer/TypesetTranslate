## Review notes and uncertainties

- The PDF OCR is sparse and corrupts mathematical glyphs, accents, and some
  headings. It was not used to settle readings.
- PDF 003, PDF 005, and PDF 011 are distinct visible title leaves. Their
  folio treatment is unnumbered in the source scan, so the map keeps them as
  separate units.
- The Contents gives the section starts and bibliography starts listed above.
  The end of each range is the page immediately before the next Contents
  boundary, with the final chapter or back-matter range ending at the visible
  last printed page. Section 3-5 is an exception in the printed Contents:
  the Contents visibly places `132` beside its title, while the section heading
  is on printed page 126 / PDF 138. Printed page 132 / PDF 144 contains the
  section's close and the bibliography heading, with bibliography entries
  continuing on printed page 133 / PDF 145. The page images control this
  boundary, and the Contents discrepancy remains recorded here.
- Figure 2-4 has a visible decimal caption label, `FIGURE 2.4`, even though
  the chapter's section and figure sequence convention is otherwise hyphenated.
- PDF 135 / printed page 123 uses a lowercase `\chi` for the second limiting
  vector in the completion argument. The native Dirac reading keeps this
  identity as `\ket{\chi}` in the limit and inner-product display; it is a
  notation conversion rather than a source correction.
- The Appendix has a single running title and two unnumbered subsections. The
  map preserves the source titles rather than inventing Appendix section
  numbers.
- The Contents prints `198` beside the Appendix bibliography, but the
  `BIBLIOGRAPHY` heading begins on printed page 199 / PDF 211. Printed page 198
  / PDF 210 contains Figure A.3 and the closing local-algebras prose. The map
  follows the page images and records the Contents discrepancy.
