\chapterbibliography

\small
\begin{thebibliography}{99}

\item[]
The axioms given in Section \ref{sec:ch3-1-axioms} are due to Gårding and Wightman in
\bibitem{pct-ch3-1} A. S. Wightman, \emph{Les Problèmes mathématiques de la théorie
quantique des champs}, pp.~11--19, Centre National de la Recherche
Scientifique, Paris, 1959; and also

\bibitem{pct-ch3-2} A. S. Wightman and L. Gårding, ``Fields as Operator-Valued
Distributions in Quantum Field Theory,'' \emph{Ark. Fys.}, \textbf{28}, 129
(1964).

\item[]
The independence of the various axioms in quantum field theory was studied by
\bibitem{pct-ch3-3} R. Haag and B. Schroer, ``The Postulates of Quantum Field Theory,''
\emph{J. Math. Phys.}, \textbf{3}, 248 (1962).

\item[]
The generalized free field was introduced by O. W. Greenberg; see for example
\bibitem{pct-ch3-4} O. W. Greenberg, ``Generalized Free Fields and Models of Local
Field Theory,'' \emph{Ann. Phys.}, \textbf{16}, 158 (1961).

\item[]
The fundamental work of D. Ruelle on collision theory referred to in
Section \ref{sec:ch3-1-axioms} is
\bibitem{pct-ch3-5} D. Ruelle, ``On the Asymptotic Condition in Quantum Field Theory,''
\emph{Helv. Phys. Acta}, \textbf{35}, 34 (1962).  Here one finds references
to earlier work of Haag and others which laid the groundwork for this form of
collision theory.  This paper also introduced the definition \eqref{eq:ch3-irreducibility} of
irreducibility as used in this book.

\item[]
Our treatment of vacuum expectation values follows
\bibitem{pct-ch3-6} A. S. Wightman, ``Quantum Field Theory in Terms of Vacuum
Expectation Values,'' \emph{Phys. Rev.}, \textbf{101}, 860 (1956).

\item[]
The important connection of the cluster decomposition property with the
uniqueness of the vacuum state was found by
\bibitem{pct-ch3-7} K. Hepp, R. Jost, D. Ruelle, and O. Steinmann, ``Necessary
Condition on Wightman Functions,'' \emph{Helv. Phys. Acta}, \textbf{34}, 542
(1961); see also

\bibitem{pct-ch3-8} H. J. Borchers, ``On the Structure of the Algebra of Field
Observables,'' \emph{Nuovo Cimento}, \textbf{24}, 214 (1962).

\item[]
The cluster decomposition theorem itself comes in many variants.  The paper
of D. Ruelle, Ref. 5, contains one, probably the most refined.  Others,
which also contain references to earlier papers, are
\bibitem{pct-ch3-9} H. Araki, ``On the Asymptotic Behavior of Vacuum Expectation
Values at Large Spacelike Separations,'' \emph{Ann. Phys.}, \textbf{11}, 260
(1960).

\bibitem{pct-ch3-10} R. Jost and K. Hepp, ``Über die Matrixelemente des Translations
Operators,'' \emph{Helv. Phys. Acta}, \textbf{35}, 34 (1962).

\bibitem{pct-ch3-11} H. Araki, K. Hepp, and D. Ruelle, ``On the Asymptotic Behavior of
Wightman Functions in Space-like Directions,'' \emph{Helv. Phys. Acta},
\textbf{35}, 164 (1962).

\item[]
The last article contains a detailed discussion of the case where there is
no mass gap.

\item[]
For a review of work on the holomorphy domains of vacuum expectation values,
see
\bibitem{pct-ch3-12} A. O. G. Källén, ``Properties of Vacuum Expectation Values of
Field Operators,'' pp.~389--447 in \emph{Dispersion Relations and Elementary
Particles}, Wiley, New York, 1960; or

\bibitem{pct-ch3-13} A. S. Wightman, ``Quantum Field Theory and Analytic Functions of
Several Complex Variables,'' \emph{Proc. Indian Math. Soc.}, \textbf{24}, 625
(1960).

\item[]
The proof of the reconstruction theorem of Section \ref{sec:ch3-reconstruction} is not as close to
the original (Ref. 6, above) as to the version of
\bibitem{pct-ch3-14} W. Schmidt and K. Baumann, ``Quantentheorie der Felder als
Distributionstheorie,'' \emph{Nuovo Cimento}, \textbf{4}, 860 (1956).

\item[]
A general survey of recent developments, including the Haag--Ruelle theory,
is to be found in
\bibitem{pct-ch3-15} A. S. Wightman, ``Recent Achievements of Axiomatic Field Theory,''
\emph{Proceedings of the Summer Seminar of IAEA}, Trieste, 1962, published as
a book, \emph{Theoretical Physics}, IAEA, Vienna, 1963.

\item[]
The most systematic approach to the non-linear program is due to K. Symanzik,
summarized in
\bibitem{pct-ch3-16} K. Symanzik, ``Green's Functions and the Quantum Theory of Fields,''
\emph{Lectures in Theoretical Physics III}, Boulder, 1960, pp.~490--531,
Interscience, New York, 1961.

\item[]
For a detailed study of the significance of the phases in the transformation
laws of fields under inversions, see
\bibitem{pct-ch3-17} G. Feinberg and S. Weinberg, ``On the Phase Factors in Inversions,''
\emph{Nuovo Cimento}, \textbf{14}, 571 (1959).

\end{thebibliography}
