20.5     Holographic correlators

The identification between the space of local operators in the N = 4 SYM and the Hilbert
space of type IIB string in the global AdS5 × S 5 can be extended to a holographic dictionary
between correlation functions of the gauge theory in the Euclidean R4 and the partition
function of string theory in EAdS5 × S 5 subject to suitable asymptotic boundary conditions.
For the sake of generalization to other examples (Chapter 21), we will describe this holo-
graphic dictionary in the context of the AdSd+1 /CFTd correspondence. The latter amounts
to the assertion that a certain theory of quantum gravity (QG) in the AdSd+1 spacetime
is equivalent to a d-dimensional conformal field theory (CFTd ). In particular, the Hilbert
space of the QG in the global AdSd+1 is identified with the Hilbert space of the CFTd on
S d−1 , or equivalently the space of local operators in the CFTd .
    By a Wick rotation and a conformal transformation, we may view a local operator O(x0 )
of the CFT, inserted at x0 ∈ Rd , as creating a state |O⟩ in the Hilbert space of the QG
associated with the totally geodesic hypersurface Sx0 ,u in EAdSd+1 defined by

                                         (x − x0 )2 + z 2 = u2 ,                               (20.47)

for any u > 0. In other words, Sx0 ,u can be viewed as the constant time slice with respect to
the Euclidean time coordinate log(u), the latter being equivalent to the Euclidean global time
by an isometry of AdSd+1 . At least at the level of a perturbative path integral formulation of
the QG, we may represent the state |O⟩ by a wave functional ΨO of field configurations on
the hypersurface Sx0 ,u . Furthermore, we can represent a correlation function of a set of local
operators O1 (x1 ), · · · , On (xn ) inserted at different points in Rd by the QG path integral in
EAdSd+1 with the regions Bxi ,ui = {(x, z) : (x−xi )2 +z 2 < u2i } excised, all the while inserting
the wave functionals ΨOi on Sxi ,ui = ∂Bxi ,ui . This is sensible so long as the ui ’s are chosen
to be sufficiently small so that the hypersurfaces Sxi ,ui do not overlap with one another. In
particular, we may take the limit ui → 0, and view the effect of integrating against the wave
functional ΨOi on Sxi ,ui as a modification of the asymptotic boundary condition of the QG
in EAdSd+1 at the point (xi , z = 0). This leads to a formal holographic dictionary

                ZQG,EAdSd+1 [O1 (x1 ), · · · , On (xn )] = ⟨O1 (x1 ) · · · On (xn )⟩CFT,Rd ,   (20.48)

where the LHS is the QG partition function with the modification of the boundary condition
at (xi , z = 0), or “boundary source”, as specified by the quantum state ΨOi . In principle
(20.48) is sensible also at the non-perturbative level, to the extent that ZQG,EAdSd+1 is defined,

say by a sort of path integral over all gravitational field and brane configurations that are
asymptotically EAdSd+1 .

using the perturbative path integral. The relevant boundary conditions for the bulk fields
and the subsequent computations of holographic correlators are discussed in Appendix R.


20.6       Supergraviton states

All quantum states of type IIB string theory in AdS5 × S 5 can be organized according to
irreducible unitary representations of the psu(2, 2|4) super-isometry or superconformal alge-
bra. The lowest weight states in such a representation are superconformal primaries, which
are states that are annihilated by the special supercharges SIα , S I α̇ (and hence also by the
special conformal generator Kµ ), and all remaining states in the representation are obtained
by acting on the primaries with QI α , QI α̇ . Special classes of superconformal primaries, also
known as chiral primaries, are such that their QI α , QI α̇ descendants obey nontrivial linear
relations (i.e. null states) and thereby give rise to shortened (BPS) representations. It is a
consequence of the superconformal algebra relation {Q, S} ∼ D + J + R that the confor-
mal weight of a chiral primary (or the energy of the corresponding state in the global AdS)
is determined by a linear combination of its SO(4) spin and SO(6) R-symmetry quantum
numbers.297
