      Consider a theory of N real scalar fields φa (a = 1, . . . , N ) in d > 0 dimensions,
with classical action S[φ], say in Euclidean spacetime. (We adopt the notation that
φ = (φ1 , . . . , φN ).) Write down the path integral expression for the generating functional

is, what does Z[J] generate and how?
       Given the Wilsonian effective action W [J] = − log Z[J], write down the quantum
effective action Γ[Φ] in terms of W [J], being sure to define Φ.
       Consider the first and second functional derivatives of Γ[Φ] and interpret how they
are related to quantities discussed above.
      Given that Z[J] may be expressed in a perturbative expansion as a sum of Feynman
diagrams, how can W [J] and Γ[Φ] be expressed in perturbative expansions? Justify your
answers mathematically.
       Assume that the classical action and the path integral measure are invariant under
