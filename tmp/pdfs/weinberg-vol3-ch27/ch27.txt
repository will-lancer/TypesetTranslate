On thisbasis,they suggested that the theory with a weak gauge coupling

the purely bosonic theory nor the simplest versions of the N = 1 and

Evidence has accumulated21 that N = 4 supersymmetric gauge theoriesare

numbers and of e with An/e. The equivalence of theorieswith large and
small coupling constants has become an increasinglyimportant theme in
stringtheory, but thisis beyond the scope of thisbook.
