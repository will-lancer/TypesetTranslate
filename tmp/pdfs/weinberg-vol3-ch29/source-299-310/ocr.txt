Itoften happens that the tree-approximation potentialin a supersymmetric
theory willtake the value zero for a continuous range of scalarfieldvalues.
(For one example, see Eq. (29.1.9).)  In thiscase the theory has a number

since supersymmetry is unbroken must be accompanied with suitable

underlying theory. Quantum effectsin the underlying theory can modify
the dependence of the effectiveLagrangian on these moduli, and even
