                                                                                               cN z N as r → 0. Thus regard the a(·; z) and φ(·, z) as being non-linear superpositions of N vortices of arbitrary
                                                                                               locations z1 ,...,zN ∈ R2 . At the very special self dual value λ = 1, single vortices can be interpreted as non-
                                                                                               interacting. At the level of the energy, the total energy of these solutions is N π which is precisely N times the energy
                                                                                               π of one vortex.
