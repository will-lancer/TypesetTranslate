1
        Provide a concise answer to the following questions:

    (i) Construct the massless representations of N ⩾ 1 supersymmetry with states labelled
        by momenta pµ and helicity λ. Find the largest values of N to have a renormalisable
        field theory (|λ| ⩽ 1) and to have one single graviton (|λ| = 2). Explain how chirality
        puts strong constraints on the number of supersymmetries. [15 marks]

 (ii) Illustrate using massive and massless N = 1 supersymmetric multiplets how the
      standard Higgs mechanism can be implemented in supersymmetric theories. How
      does this differ from the super Higgs effect? [10 marks]

(iii) Explain how in N = 1 theories for chiral superfields the strength of the quartic
      coupling of scalar fields g2 |ϕ|4 and that of the Yukawa couplings gϕψψ are determined
      by the same parameter g. You may use the Wess Zumino model to illustrate this.
      Explain in a few words how this may be relevant to address the hierarchy problem
      for the Higgs mass. [10 marks]

(iv) Using the N = 1 supersymmetry algebra show that the number of bosons equals the
     number of fermions in any multiplet. Prove also that the energy is not negative.
     What can we conclude from this result regarding the value of the energy when
     supersymmetry is broken? [15 marks]

 (v) Define the supersymmetry transformation of a general scalar superfield and prove
     that the product of two superfields is a superfield. Is S(x, θ, θ̄) = φ(x) a superfield?
     Explain. [10 marks]
